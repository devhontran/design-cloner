Source: https://rly.serious.business/ (RLY Network, a Web3 protocol for launching social/creator tokens; designed by Huy Phan with Serious Business; Awwwards Site of the Day, Nov 4, 2022 + Developer Award). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A precise, quiet dark interface for a crypto protocol. Charcoal space drawn with **thin overlapping circles** (like Venn diagrams / orbits), light geometric type, tiny caps buttons and one acid-lime signal colour.
- The audience is developers and consumer-app founders. The one idea is **"token economies made easy"**: clarity and calm in a noisy category, with geometry as the metaphor for connected economies.

## Composition & layout
- **Nav:** "RLY Network" logo (hexagon mark, bold "RLY" + light "Network") at left. Small links at right (Home · About · Community · Developer Resources · News | Contact, ~10px). The active item is lime with a small dot under it.
- **Hero:** three big **hairline circles** (1px, `#272727`) overlapping horizontally across the viewport. The centre circle holds a small grey kicker ("RLY Protocol powers consumer apps"), a 2-line headline (~40px, weight 400, "Token economies made easy."), and two CTAs: an underlined caps text link "START BUILDING IN 3 STEPS" and a **small white rectangular caps button** "READ THE DOCUMENTATION" (turns lime on hover). The side circles hold **stat columns**: "350+ Total TBCs ↗", "21MM+ Network Transactions", "195MM sRLY+ …" with tiny units set as superscript caps and a short dash separator.
- A full-width hairline separates sections, with "SCROLL FOR MORE" in tiny caps centred and a white **cursor dot** that follows the mouse.
- **Features:** a lime outlined pill tag "OUR FEATURES" with a small circular icon badge. A 3-line headline where a **tiny grey paragraph is inset inside the last line** ("Use our powerful Web3 software suite…" sits before "tokenized venture"), and a small white caps button "DEVELOPER RESOURCES: LET'S BUILD!" at the end of the line.
- Numbered feature rows (01 circle, "Seamlessly launch social tokens", with a description at right) separated by hairlines.

## Typography voice
- One geometric grotesk (HK Guise; substitute Urbanist) at 400–500: headlines are light and airy, sentence case, tight tracking (−0.03em), 40–68px.
- Micro UI is 8–10px **uppercase** with tracking (buttons, labels, "SCROLL FOR MORE").
- Numbers are large with tiny superscript unit caps ("21MM+", "195MM sRLY+").

## Color usage
- About 70% charcoal (`#121212` / `#1a1a1a`), 15% white text, 10% greys (`#909090` secondary, `#272727` lines), 5% acid lime `#ceff45` (active nav, tags, button hover, badges).

## Imagery & iconography
- Almost no imagery: geometry (hairline circles), small circular icon badges, and ↗ arrows on stats.
- A cursor dot follows the mouse and scales on links.

## Signature details
1. **Overlapping hairline circles** spanning the hero, with content inside each.
2. **Stat columns flanking the headline** with superscript unit caps and ↗ links.
3. **Tiny white rectangular caps buttons** (lime on hover) next to underlined caps text links.
4. **Paragraph inset inside the headline line** plus an inline button at the line end.
5. **Lime outlined pill tags** with circular badges.

## Interaction & motion
- Crisp in-out easing (`cubic-bezier(0.85, 0, 0.15, 1)`, ~0.3s). Circles subtly rotate and scale on scroll. Headlines reveal by line. Stats count up.
- Buttons fill lime on hover, and the cursor dot grows over interactive items.

## Anti-patterns
- No gradients, glows or neon beyond the single lime. No rainbow crypto imagery or 3D coins.
- No heavy bold headlines. Keep light and precise.
- No rounded buttons (tags are pills, buttons are rectangles).
- No photography.
