Source: https://terminal-industries.com (Terminal Industries, an AI "Yard Operating System" for logistics yards; Awwwards Site of the Month, Sep 2025). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A freight yard is re-imagined as a sci-fi control room. A cinematic trucking film opens into a black space where trucks and yards dissolve into **wireframe point clouds**, then settles into a clean white enterprise page with an acid-lime signal colour.
- The audience is logistics and supply-chain executives. The one idea is **"the yard of the future starts today"**: industrial reality (trucks at dusk) translated into computer vision (wireframes, grids) and sold with calm SaaS clarity.

## Composition & layout
- A **floating translucent nav capsule** (rounded ~8px, grey glass, inset ~64px from edges) holds the logo, text menus with ⌄, a mono "✦ Ask Terminal →" search pill, a phone icon button, and three small mono caps buttons: **lime "EXPLORE PRODUCT"**, white "REQUEST DEMO" and grey "CONTACT US".
- **Hero:** a full-bleed video of a truck silhouette at dusk (orange sky, dark ground) with a tiny mono "SCROLL TO EXPLORE". It transitions to a **black starfield with a 3D point-cloud truck and yard**, with white statements ("our gate and your dock...") lower right.
- **Section edges are notched:** dark sections end with a subtle trapezoid tab or notch cut into the boundary (the white section below has a raised centre lip). The same notch shape appears on cards (the calculator result card has notched top corners).
- White sections have centred headlines (~46–70px, weight 400, tracking −0.05em, deep teal `#052424`) and short grey kickers ("Yard Efficiency Calculator", "Why Terminal").
- **Cards:** light grey `#f0f0f0` panels, radius ~12–20px. Each has a mono caps index label ("01 FAST START"), a 20px title, a paragraph, an aerial yard photo, and a grey full-width button.
- **Interactive calculator:** inputs with underlines at left, and a deep-teal result card with a big "$ 641,626" at right.
- Logo wall on a hairline grid with + crosshair marks at intersections. A testimonial sits over a full-bleed snowy truck photo.
- **Footer:** deep teal `#052424`, lime logo mark, "Gartner" badge, and mono caps column heads.

## Typography voice
- Suisse Int'l (substitute Inter) at 400–500. Headlines are sentence case, **tight tracking (−0.05em)**, line-height 0.95–1.0, 2 lines, centred. Body is 16–18px, line-height 1.4.
- **Geist Mono** for micro-labels and buttons: 9–11px UPPERCASE with wide tracking (+0.12em) ("01 FAST START", "AT THE GATE", "TECHNOLOGY").
- Headline reveals often fade words from grey to ink ("One Modular Platform ~~Infinite Possibilities~~" with the first half light grey).

## Color usage
- About 55% white, 15% deep teal `#052424` (text, dark cards, footer), 15% imagery (dusk orange, night black), 10% light grey `#f0f0f0` surfaces, 5% **acid lime `#abff02`**.
- Lime is the signal colour: the primary CTA, logo mark, the "✦" in Ask Terminal, highlights on active grid lines and inline emphasis words. Never use it for backgrounds larger than a button.
- Black is only for the point-cloud/space sections.

## Imagery & iconography
- Cinematic trucking footage (dusk silhouettes, snowy highways) and aerial drone shots of container yards, with rounded 8–12px corners in cards.
- **3D point-cloud and wireframe renders** of trucks, trailers and yard grids on black with star particles: the "AI vision" layer.
- Thin 1px line icons (phone, arrows, social) and + crosshair markers on grid intersections.

## Signature details
1. **Floating glass nav capsule** with three stacked-caps mono buttons, one in acid lime.
2. **Point-cloud / wireframe truck sequences** on a black starfield between real footage.
3. **Notched section boundaries and card corners** (trapezoid tabs).
4. **Mono caps micro-labels with index numbers** on every card and column.
5. **Deep teal + acid lime** on white: teal for weight, lime for signal.

## Interaction & motion
- Scroll-driven film: video → point cloud → white page. Headlines reveal word by word from grey to ink. Cards slide up in a staggered sequence.
- The calculator updates live. FAQ items open with a + → × toggle, and tabs are mono outline chips.
- Easing is standard ease-out at 0.4–0.6s. Hover darkens buttons and nudges arrows.

## Anti-patterns
- No colourful multi-hue palettes or gradients on UI. Only teal, lime, white and greys.
- No heavy bold headlines and no all-caps display. Caps are only for mono micro-labels.
- No playful illustration. Imagery is real footage or technical wireframes.
- Don't use pill buttons. Buttons are small rounded rectangles (≈4px) with mono caps labels.
