Source: https://watson.la/ (Watson, a Los Angeles brand and entertainment creative studio behind film campaigns such as Dune: Part Three and The Odyssey; Awwwards Site of the Month, Sep 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A title card from a film festival programme. One centred sentence is set as a typographic collage of mixed faces, with tiny images embedded between words and footnote numbers linking to sections.
- The audience is film studios and entertainment brands. The one idea is **"a brand & entertainment creative studio, led by craft, in search of conversation"**: the manifesto sentence *is* the website, and everything else is footnotes.

## Composition & layout
- A white page with a minimal header: "Watson" top-left, a centred footnote nav "⁽¹⁾Partnerships, ⁽²⁾Capabilities, ⁽³⁾Conversations" (superscript numbers), and "About" top-right, all ~11px.
- **Hero:** a small "LA" label, the giant **condensed bold wordmark "WATSON"** (~320px wide-set caps, centred), then the manifesto sentence centred over 6–7 lines at ~48–56px: "(Los Angeles) is a / brand & / entertainment⁽¹⁾ / creative [img] studio, / led by craft⁽²⁾ — / in [img] search of / conversation⁽³⁾".
- **Inline image chips** (~48×48 squares with portraits, sneakers, objects) sit between words and cycle through different images as you scroll.
- Key words are **underlined** and carry superscript footnote numbers that match the nav.
- Under the sentence is a small link "More (About) Us →". The bottom band has 3 columns of tiny text: a greyscale event photo labelled "CURRENTLY CELEBRATING OUR GRAND CLIO AWARD" at left, "(Recently)" news, and "(Contact)" details.

## Typography voice
- **Three voices in one sentence**: a thin light grotesk ("(Los Angeles)"), a bold grotesk ("is a"), and an editorial serif ("brand &", "entertainment", "creative studio"), mixed word by word. Sizes are equal and the faces alternate.
- The wordmark is heavy condensed caps (substitute Archivo Narrow 700 / Oswald).
- Labels use **parentheses** "(Recently)", "(Contact)", "(About)". Footnotes use superscript ⁽¹⁾⁽²⁾⁽³⁾ and em dashes.
- Small text is 11–12px regular with tight leading.

## Color usage
- About 90% white and black type. Colour comes only from the small inline images (red portraits, sneakers) and occasional brown `#70412a` in media.
- No brand colour. Black underlines are the only emphasis device besides the typeface change.

## Imagery & iconography
- Tiny square image chips inline with text, cycling (portraits, product shots, a crow illustration). One greyscale event photo in the footer area.
- Icons are only small arrows →.

## Signature details
1. **Centred manifesto sentence as hero**, mixing serif, bold and thin grotesk word by word.
2. **Inline image chips** between words that cycle on scroll.
3. **Footnote navigation** with superscript numbers linking sentence words to sections.
4. **Giant condensed WATSON wordmark** above the sentence.
5. **Parenthesised labels** and tiny 3-column info band at the bottom.

## Interaction & motion
- Quick, understated (≈0.2s). Inline images swap as you scroll or hover. Underlines animate on hover. Footnote links jump to sections.
- The sentence stays pinned while images cycle.

## Anti-patterns
- No big hero images, video backgrounds or card grids.
- No colour UI and no buttons with fills. Use text links with arrows.
- Don't use one typeface for the manifesto. The mix is the identity.
- Don't left-align the manifesto. It is centred.
