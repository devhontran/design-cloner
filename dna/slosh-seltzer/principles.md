Source: https://sloshseltzer.com/ (Slosh Seltzer, a fictional hard seltzer brand site by a design studio, "not a real alcohol product"; Awwwards Site of the Month, Jun 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend. WebGL site: capture needs a hardware-like GPU name.

## Essence
- A party in a can. Bubblegum pink and cherry red on the age gate flip to kelly green with pink stickers, confetti glyphs and a photoreal 3D can top you "pop" to start.
- The audience is fun-seeking adults and the design community. The one idea is **"the big kids' table"**: grown-up drink, childlike spirit. Loud poster typography, sticker culture and toy-like interaction.

## Composition & layout
- **Age gate:** a flat pink `#ffc1ff` field with a small "SLOSH" logo top-centre, a giant 2-line headline "THIS IS THE / BIG KIDS TABLE" (~100px ultra-condensed caps, cherry red `#ff0837`, centred), a 10px mono line under it, and a red pill "I'M READY TO PARTY". Scattered red lightning bolts, sparkle and a smiley doodle float around. The disclaimer line sits at the bottom.
- **Hero:** a kelly green `#00a165` field with "SLOSH" and "SELTZER" as enormous **inline-striped letters** (outline + pink fill stripes, ~200px) placed diagonally (top-left, bottom-right). A **photoreal 3D can top** sits centred between them, with a curved arrow and a pink tag "POP THE TOP". Also: a rotating circular badge "4 FLAVORS" with cherries, a starburst sticker "100% GOOD TIMES", a rotated caps line "GET THE PARTY STARTED", squiggles and eyes doodles.
- Small **sticker squares** (pink tiles with icons) confetti across the screen continuously.
- Compositions are poster-like and asymmetric, with elements at angles (−30° to +30°).

## Typography voice
- **Ultra-condensed heavy caps** (Steelfish; substitute Big Shoulders Display 800) for all headlines and buttons. Very tall, tight, 100–200px.
- Headline letters get an **inline stripe treatment** (outlined letter with a second inner outline and partial pink fill), like retro can labels.
- Supporting text is a small grotesk/mono (FK Grotesk; substitute Space Mono) at 10–12px, lowercase sentence case with bold key words ("this is **not a real** alcohol product").
- Circular badge text runs around a circle.

## Color usage
- Two palettes swap by state. **Gate:** pink `#ffc1ff` bg + cherry red `#ff0837` type. **Site:** kelly green `#00a165` bg + pink `#ffc1ff` type and stickers.
- Hot pinks (`#ffabe7`, `#ff79b1`) are used as tints. No neutrals except the silver of the 3D can.

## Imagery & iconography
- One photoreal 3D can (brushed aluminium top, pink rim) as the interactive centrepiece.
- Flat doodle icons: lightning bolts, smileys, eyes, cherries, starbursts, squiggles, sparkles, all single-colour.
- Pink sticker tiles with tiny line icons, spinning as confetti.

## Signature details
1. **Age gate as brand moment**: pink field, giant red condensed caps, "I'M READY TO PARTY" pill.
2. **Inline-striped condensed wordmarks** placed diagonally around the 3D can.
3. **"Pop the top" interactive 3D can** with a hand-drawn curved arrow and tag.
4. **Sticker confetti and doodles** (bolts, smileys, cherries, starbursts) everywhere.
5. **Rotating circular badge text** ("4 FLAVORS").

## Interaction & motion
- Playful and springy (`cubic-bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)`, ~0.6s). Stickers rain and rotate. The badge spins slowly.
- Clicking the can triggers the pop and fizz animation that reveals flavours. The cursor pushes confetti around.

## Anti-patterns
- No neutral greys, black backgrounds or corporate layouts.
- No thin or wide type, and no serif body. Condensed caps + small mono only.
- No photography of people. Use 3D can + doodles.
- Nothing perfectly aligned. Tilt and scatter elements.
