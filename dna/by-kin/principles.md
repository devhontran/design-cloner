Source: https://by-kin.com/ ('kin, a creative commercial interiors, branding and graphic design studio in the UK; designed by Huy Phan; Awwwards Site of the Day, Dec 26, 2024 + Developer Award). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A friendly, confident studio that keeps the home page almost empty. A big coral monospace-style wordmark "'kin", one sentence, three thumbnails and a hand-drawn crowd of the team waving a foam finger.
- The audience is workplace, retail and hospitality clients. The one idea is **"people first, well-made spaces"**: warm, human and a bit cheeky, with generous whitespace and a playful layout switch.

## Composition & layout
- **Home, layout 1:** a warm off-white `#f4f2ed` single screen. The coral wordmark "'kin" is huge top-left (~130px tall; the apostrophe is a coral tick, the i-dot a coral circle). Nav is top-right as **underlined** text links (About · Work · Journal · Contact, ~16px). The 4-line statement is mid-left ("'kin are a creative commercial interiors, branding and graphic design studio.", ~16px medium). "FEATURED WORKS" sits at bottom-left with 3 small thumbnails (01/02/03). The line-drawn team illustration is bottom-right, and email/social links are in tiny underlined mono caps.
- **Layout toggle:** "LAYOUT (1) 2" with the active number in a thin circle. **Layout 2** rearranges the same elements: statement top-left, wordmark centred larger, nav stacked in big underlined type (~28px) top-right, and thumbnails in a row bottom-left.
- **Work page:** a full-screen dark photo of the active project, with a **stacked centred list of project names** (~64px) where the active name is **coral** and the others are ghost white at ~20%. A tiny mono category "INTERIOR DESIGN" overlaps the active name. A filter list in mono caps sits at the left ("ALL(13) / INTERIOR DESIGN(9) / GRAPHIC & BRAND IDENTITY(5)").

## Typography voice
- **Apercu Pro** (substitute DM Sans) at 500 for the statement, nav and project names, in sentence case.
- **Apercu Mono** (substitute DM Mono) in **9–10px uppercase** for labels (LAYOUT, FEATURED WORKS, FILTER, dates, emails).
- The wordmark is a geometric monospace-like lowercase with coral punctuation.
- Links are underlined, including big nav items.

## Color usage
- About 60% warm off-white `#f4f2ed`, 20% near-black `#111214`, 20% coral `#fe6e4e` (wordmark, active project name, illustration accents).
- Coral is the only accent. The illustration is black line + coral spots.

## Imagery & iconography
- A hand-drawn black line illustration of the team (with coral details: foam finger, logo t-shirts).
- Interior photography (offices, canteens, retail) as small square thumbnails on the home page and full-bleed darkened on the work page.
- Icons: thin circle for the active layout number, and underlines.

## Signature details
1. **Huge coral "'kin" wordmark** with a tick apostrophe and dot.
2. **Layout 1/2 toggle** that rearranges the home composition.
3. **Underlined nav links**, big in layout 2.
4. **Line-drawn team illustration** with coral accents.
5. **Stacked project-name index** over a full-bleed photo, with the active name coral and the rest ghosted.

## Interaction & motion
- Slow, dramatic in-out easing (`cubic-bezier(0.76, 0, 0.24, 1)`, ~1.2s) when switching layouts: elements glide to new positions.
- On the work page, scrolling the name list swaps the background photo. Hover underlines thicken.

## Anti-patterns
- No dense sections or card grids on the home page. Keep it one calm screen.
- No second accent colour, gradients or shadows.
- No bold headlines. Weight 500 max.
- Don't drop the illustration and mono labels. They carry the personality.
