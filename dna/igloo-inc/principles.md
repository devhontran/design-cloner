## Essence
- A cold, silent Arctic scene built in real time. A wireframe lattice assembles into an igloo of glowing ice blocks on a snowy peak, the blocks detach and float as data points, and prismatic glitches tear the frame between chapters.
- The audience is investors and the web3 community. The one idea is **"building the future, block by block"**: the igloo as a company made of ventures (blocks), rendered with near-monochrome restraint and a HUD-like data layer.

## Composition & layout
- A full-viewport WebGL scene with a scroll-driven camera. No classic page sections.
- **Build sequence:** a floating grey void with a wireframe network of lines and nodes. An igloo draws itself (wire → glowing shell → solid ice blocks with bright seams), then the terrain (a snowy mountain) forms beneath it and the camera flies in.
- **Blocks as data:** individual ice blocks lift off the igloo, labelled with tiny numbers and connected by hairlines, like nodes in a graph.
- **HUD text blocks:** small **inverted text chips** (white rectangles with dark text, or dark bars with light text) stacked top-left under the logo and top-right as a paragraph. They appear as glitchy, pixel-revealed blocks.
- The wordmark "iGLOO" in a **pixel-outline** style sits top-left with a small inverted label under it.
- Transitions use **prismatic / chromatic glitch** (rainbow light leaks and datamosh smears) between chapters.

## Typography voice
- A custom **pixel outline wordmark** (double-stroke rounded pixel letters). Substitute: Silkscreen or a pixel font with outline.
- The HUD uses a small pixel/mono type (substitute Space Mono), 9–11px UPPERCASE, set on solid inverted rectangles. Text is revealed character by character.
- There are no large display headlines. The 3D scene carries the scale.

## Color usage
- About 95% cool greys and whites (`#a0a5b1` fog, `#d6dae2` snow, white glow, `#12161e` shadow/HUD ink). A monochrome, almost colourless palette.
- Colour appears only in **prismatic glitch flares** (rainbow spectrum) during transitions, a rare and intense accent.

## Imagery & iconography
- Real-time 3D: wireframe lattices, ice blocks with emissive seams, a photoreal snowy mountain, fog, and particle snow.
- Hairline connectors with node dots and tiny numeric labels.
- No icons. Only HUD rectangles and lines.

## Signature details
1. **Wireframe-to-solid build** of a single iconic object (the igloo) as the hero narrative.
2. **Glowing seams** between ice blocks, and blocks detaching as labelled data nodes.
3. **Inverted HUD text chips** (white-on-dark / dark-on-white rectangles) revealed with pixel glitches.
4. **Pixel-outline wordmark.**
5. **Prismatic glitch transitions** as the only colour.

## Interaction & motion
- Slow, cinematic, scroll-scrubbed camera with damped easing. Particles drift constantly.
- Text blocks decode or glitch in (random characters settle). Chapter changes fire a short chromatic datamosh.
- Hover on blocks highlights connections. Provide a reduced-motion still frame of the finished igloo.

## Anti-patterns
- No saturated brand colour in UI. Keep it arctic monochrome.
- No regular web layout (cards, grids, hero text blocks).
- No large headings in sans or serif. Scale belongs to the 3D.
- Don't overuse the rainbow glitch. It's a transition accent only.
