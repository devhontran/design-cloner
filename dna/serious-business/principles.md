Source: https://serious.business/ (Serious Business, a premium branding agency for B2B tech scaleups, Munich/Stockholm; Huy Phan is their designer/collaborator; Awwwards Site of the Day, Sep 17, 2024 + Developer Award + Business & Services Honors). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A B2B agency that refuses to be grey. A **bubblegum pink** page, a heavy caps wordmark with a smiley, and stacked service slabs in lilac, white, sunflower and black, with a wink in everything ("brands for scaleups that make people smile").
- The audience is tech scaleup founders and marketers. The one idea is **"serious work, not serious attitude"**: rigorous grid and product-grade mock-ups wrapped in candy colour and humour.

## Composition & layout
- **Chrome:** a white pill "Let's work" top-left, the "SERIOUS.BUSINESS" logo centre, and a white pill "Menu" plus a **round arrow button** top-right.
- **Hero:** pink `#fbc1d4` with the huge heavy caps wordmark "SERIOUS.BUSINESS" spanning the full width at the top (~120px, weight 800), a centred sub-caption in **serif** ("Premium Branding Agency / for B2B Tech Scaleups"), an embossed pink smiley "SB" circle in the centre, and a small video thumbnail bottom-left.
- **Statement:** a big sentence right-aligned (~40px): "Crafting premium brands for scaleups that make people smile."
- **Services:** **full-width stacked slabs** that pin and stack on scroll, each a different colour (lilac `#c3abff` Brand Strategy, white Visual Identity, sunflower `#fed35b` Website, black Product). Each has a big title (~64px, weight 400), a serif description at left, a small list of deliverables in the middle, and a mock-up image at right (rounded ~6px).
- **Work:** a 2-column grid of project images (rounded 6px) with a bold name + "|" + tagline and tiny grey tags ("Strategy – Visual Identity – Website"). A white pill "See more projects ←".
- **Clients:** a black section with "In a serious relationships with:" in serif at left and white client logos in a grid.
- **Contact:** two big blocks side by side, a black "Send Request" (with "You feel it too? Let's talk, no strings attached") and a lilac "Brand Masterplan" offer.
- **Footer:** pink, with tiny caps labels (EXPLORE, STALK US, SAY HELLO, EXCEPTIONAL TALENT?), link lists at ~16px, and the giant wordmark with an **outlined smiley replacing the dot** ("SERIOUS ☺ BUSINESS") at the bottom.

## Typography voice
- **PP Mori** (substitute Manrope) at 400–600 for headlines and body, sentence case, tight tracking. **Heavy 800 caps** only for the wordmark.
- **Nib Pro serif** (substitute Newsreader) for sub-captions, descriptions and the "In a serious relationships with:" line, giving an editorial counterpoint.
- Micro labels are 7–9px caps.

## Color usage
- About 55% bubblegum pink `#fbc1d4`, 25% near-black `#1e1e1e`, and 20% service slab colours: lilac `#c3abff`, sunflower `#fed35b`, white, hot pink `#ff7ec4` (buttons such as "Accept all").
- Each service or offer gets its own flat slab colour. The rest is pink.

## Imagery & iconography
- Product-grade mock-ups (laptops, phones, billboards, packaging, 3D shoe renders) on neutral or colour backgrounds, with soft 6px radius.
- The smiley motif (SB smiley, smiley in the wordmark). Round arrow buttons ← →.

## Signature details
1. **Bubblegum pink base** with a **heavy caps wordmark** whose dot becomes a **smiley**.
2. **White pill buttons paired with a round arrow button** ("Menu" + ←).
3. **Stacked sticky service slabs** in lilac / white / sunflower / black.
4. **Serif sub-captions** under grotesk headlines.
5. **Black + lilac twin CTA blocks** ("Send Request" / "Brand Masterplan").

## Interaction & motion
- `cubic-bezier(0.5, 1, 0.89, 1)` at ~0.5s. Service slabs stack like cards on scroll. The hero smiley embosses with the cursor. Pills invert on hover, and arrow buttons rotate.
- Project images zoom subtly on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No corporate blue or grey. The base stays pink.
- No thin serif headlines. Serif is only for supporting copy.
- No sharp rectangular buttons. Use pills and circles.
- Don't lose the humour (smiley, playful copy).
