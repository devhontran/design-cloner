Source: https://sondaven.com/en (Son Daven, a resort design hotel and investment project in Yaremche, Carpathians, Ukraine; Awwwards Site of the Month, Jun 2026). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It reads like a limited-edition field journal about a mountain legend. It is a **two-colour world, sand `#a89474` and umber `#2c2824`**, where photographs of timber-and-stone terraces dissolve into vertical-bar textures like wind through pine trunks.
- The audience is investors and buyers of hotel units. The one idea is **"a place where the mountains speak"**: ancient Hutsul roots told through classical roman capitals, with a modern data layer (yield, timelines, forecast) set in tiny wide technical caps.

## Composition & layout
- The hero is a full-bleed photo of the resort at sunrise. There is a heavy extended wordmark top-left, two headline stats top-right ("UP TO 10%" / "UP TO 30%" with tiny captions), a 3-line roman caps title bottom-left, a meta row of 3 small spec blocks, and a **circle outline CTA** ("INVEST IN SON DAVEN", ~90px) bottom-right.
- The page alternates **sand and umber full-bleed fields**. Transitions between them are made of **vertical bars** (1–4px lines) forming mountain ridges, forests or a sun disc, a barcode-like dissolve.
- Section titles are giant roman caps **split to opposite edges** of the page ("CONSTRUCTION ······ TIMELINE", "PROJECT ······ GALLERY", "F · A · Q" spread across the width) with a full-width photo band immediately below.
- Headline compositions mix scale inside one phrase: "THE / MOUNTAINS / WHERE / SPEAK", with a small "THE" and 2–3 staggered lines at different indents.
- The gallery "Why Son Daven captivates" scatters **photo cards rotated ±2–4°** on the sand field, each with a small caption bottom-left and a white `+` circle bottom-right, drifting horizontally on scroll.
- Data blocks: umber cards with 1px sand rules (timeline rows "CONSTRUCTION BEGINS · IN Q4 2025"), stat rows of 4 roman numerals (20+ · 2 · 37 · 7) with 3-line wide-caps captions, and FAQ as numbered rows (`04`, `05` …) with `+` at right.
- A tiny barcode progress indicator is fixed bottom-left on every screen.

## Typography voice
- Display is a flared classical roman (KTF Metro Roman; substitute Marcellus), **UPPERCASE, weight 400**, 32–144px, line-height 0.95–1.0, centred or split. Numbers (stats, "10%") are also in the roman.
- Body and labels use an **extra-wide technical sans** (KTF Metro Blueline; substitute Michroma) at **9–12px UPPERCASE**, tracking about +0.08em, line-height 1.6. Even paragraphs are set this way in narrow columns (≈ 35ch).
- The wordmark "SON DAVEN" is a heavy extended grotesk with horizontal-line inlines. Treat it as a logo, not a typeface for headlines.
- Kickers are centred between dot triplets: `••• UNIQUENESS •••`, `••• DEVELOPER •••`.
- Quotes are roman caps in curly quotes, centred, 3–4 lines.

## Color usage
- About 50% umber `#2c2824`, 40% sand `#a89474`, 10% photography (warm dusk light, forest greens, stone). Text is sand on umber and umber on sand; it is always one of the two.
- Near-white `#f4efe6` is used only for the tiny `+` circles on photo cards. There is no other colour. The accent is the swap between the two tones.
- Photos are colour-graded warm and slightly desaturated so they sit inside the duotone.

## Imagery & iconography
- Architecture renders (terraced timber blocks with green roofs, pools, forest), Hutsul culture (bonfires, kids on rocks, crafts), interiors (wood, stone, round windows).
- Vertical-bar halftone illustrations of mountains, forest and sun used as section dividers and backgrounds, with a "HOLD AND MOVE" draggable sun control.
- Icons are thin-line circles with `+`, arrows `‹ ›` in 20px circles, a small monogram glyph (the SD rune), and a map with hairline paths and small labelled pins.

## Signature details
1. **Sand/umber duotone** with no third UI colour.
2. **Vertical-bar dissolves**: barcode-like line fields forming landscapes between sections.
3. **Split giant roman headlines** pinned to the left and right page edges with a photo band beneath.
4. **Tilted scattered photo cards** (±3°) with `+` circles on the sand field.
5. **Wide technical micro-caps** (9–12px, extended) for all body and data, plus `••• KICKER •••` labels and the **circle outline "INVEST" CTA**.

## Interaction & motion
- Slow, smooth scroll (Lenis) with `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` at 0.6–1.2s. Bars sweep in to reveal the next colour field. Cards drift and rotate slightly with scroll.
- Headline words slide from their edges toward position. Photos parallax inside their frames.
- Buttons are small pills (umber on sand, sand on dark) that invert on hover. Circle CTAs rotate their text or fill on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No white or grey backgrounds and no pure black. The UI only uses sand and umber.
- No sans-serif headlines and no lowercase display text.
- No rounded cards and no shadows. The only rounded things are pill buttons and circles.
- No dense UI grids. Each viewport shows one idea (a title, a gallery, a data block).
- No bright accent colours, gradients or glass effects. Texture comes from the line fields.
