Source: https://madeinuxstudio.com/ (MIUX — Made In UX Studio, a brand & digital design studio in Seattle; designed by Huy Phan; Awwwards Site of the Day, Aug 3, 2025 + Developer Award). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A calm, warm-toned boutique studio: linen paper, taupe blocks, soft natural-light lifestyle photography (a woman working in a sunlit café, flowers, teams on sofas) and a handwritten "m" signature logo.
- The audience is founders and growing businesses. The one idea is **"premium design for brands ready to be trusted, chosen and remembered"**: quiet confidence, editorial spacing and hairline structure.

## Composition & layout
- **Header:** a hairline bar with the script logo at left and a tiny tagline ("A studio crafting captivating digital experiences"). At right, a **4-cell nav** where each cell has a tiny grey kicker above and a label + → ("Projects (11) / Work →", "Work with us / Services →", "Meet MIUX / About Us →", "Get in touch / Contact →"), separated by vertical hairlines.
- **Hero:** a split 60/40 layout. A taupe `#d5c8b0` panel on the left with a 4-line light headline (~40px, "Premium design for brands ready to be trusted, chosen, and remembered"), a small photo of the office sign, a short grey blurb and "Limited Project Availability For Q2–Q3". A full-height lifestyle photo on the right.
- **Mission:** a bullet kicker "• Our mission" + a ~26px statement, with underlined links "Service Pricing →" and "Inquire →".
- **Services:** hairline columns, each with a **giant thin numeral** (~110px "1", "2", "3"), service name (~26px), a tiny description, and a hover photo card filling the column (with a round → button).
- **Latest Achievements:** a full-bleed dark photo with a **circular dial carousel** (a thin circle with tick marks, "/01…/04" counters, the project image in a round mask at centre, a round "→ View Project" button, and "Scroll" below). The project name sits at left in white (~20px).
- **Client feedback:** a full-bleed floral photo with **tilted testimonial cards** (white polaroid-like cards with portrait, name • role, and logo) stacked and shuffling.
- **Footer:** a taupe CTA band ("Let's bring your vision to life", large light type that sharpens from blur), then a hairline grid: the big script logo at left, numbered nav rows (01 Home … 05 Contact Us) at right, and bottom cells for Contact / Subscribe (email field →) / Connect / Others.

## Typography voice
- One grotesk at **light 300–400**, slightly condensed feel, sentence case, tight tracking (−0.02em). Headlines are 26–40px, and numerals are huge and thin.
- Micro text is 9–11px grey, with bullet kickers "• Our mission", "• Client Feedback".
- The script signature logo is the only decorative type.

## Color usage
- About 50% linen `#f7f3ed`, 15% taupe/sand `#d5c8b0`, 20% photography (warm, natural light), 10% charcoal text `#333335`, 5% bronze `#986c32` and greys.
- There is no saturated UI colour. Warmth comes from photos and taupe blocks.

## Imagery & iconography
- Lifestyle and editorial photography (natural light, plants, cafés, flowers, candid team shots) and product mock-ups (phones, laptops) in real settings. Rounded ~8–12px for inset photos, full-bleed for sections.
- Round white buttons with →, and thin circles/dials.

## Signature details
1. **Hairline-divided 4-cell nav** with a grey kicker above each label.
2. **Giant thin numerals** heading service columns.
3. **Circular dial carousel** for featured projects over a dark full-bleed photo.
4. **Tilted testimonial polaroid cards** over a floral photo.
5. **Script "m" signature logo** and a numbered footer nav grid.

## Interaction & motion
- `cubic-bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)` at ~0.55s. The dial rotates between projects on scroll. Testimonial cards shuffle. The CTA text blurs into focus. Service columns reveal photos on hover.
- Links underline and arrows nudge on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No bold or heavy type, and no all-caps headlines.
- No saturated colours or dark-mode pages (dark only inside photo sections).
- No boxed cards with shadows. Structure is hairlines.
- No stock-looking cold imagery. Keep it warm and natural.
