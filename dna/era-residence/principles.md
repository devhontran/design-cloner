Source: https://www.era-residence.com/ (ERA Residence, a gated residential project in Estepona, Costa del Sol; Awwwards Site of the Month, Aug 2026). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It reads like a Mediterranean resort brochure printed on cream paper. Tall, razor-thin Didone capitals sit against sun-drenched photography of white villas, pools and bougainvillea.
- The audience is buyers of second homes. The one idea is **"a place to live, return year after year"**: calm, sunny and ceremonial, with architecture framed by arches and circles.

## Composition & layout
- The page is a sequence of full-bleed, full-height photographs (sky, villas, pool at dusk, interiors) alternating with cream `#f3f3ec` pauses. Photos carry most of the page.
- A fixed left rail holds a circular badge logo top-left (flower mark ringed by "ERA · RESIDENCE" set on a circle) and a vertical `SCROLL` label with a 1px line and a `00` section counter.
- Top-right nav stacks "SELECT / AN APARTMENT" in the condensed serif (2 lines, ~20px) over tiny extended caps links (`BOOK A CALL`, `CONTACT`).
- Cream pauses are centred: a 10px caps kicker ("A PLACE TO LIVE — TO RETURN YEAR AFTER YEAR"), a 1px vertical line ≈90px tall, then a giant condensed serif word ("DEVELOPER", "ARCHITECTURE").
- A **huge arch** (a semicircle-topped panel in powder blue `#b5cedb`, ~85% of viewport width) rises over the photo and holds curved-set text ("THREE REASONS TO CHOOSE ERA") and a centred flower mark.
- Feature lists sit over photos on the right with a 1px left rule, in serif caps at ~24px (GATED COMMUNITY / SWIMMING POOL / SPA & GYM …).
- Footer is solid aubergine `#340c24`: a centred flower mark, the phone number set as a ~90px condensed Didone headline, the address in 10px caps, and legal links in the corners.

## Typography voice
- Display is an ultra-condensed high-contrast Didone (Ambroise Francois Std Bold Condensed; free substitute Bodoni Moda, set tighter and narrower where possible), **always UPPERCASE**, 36–173px, line-height 0.87–1.0. Hairline serifs and thick verticals give the "fashion masthead" feel.
- Body and labels use a wide/extended grotesk (Maison Neue Extended; substitute Archivo at wdth 125). Labels are 10px bold caps with +0.05em tracking. Short descriptive paragraphs are 12–14px sentence case, centred, and ≤ 45ch.
- Some headlines follow a curve (text on an arc along the arch top). The logo text runs around a circle.
- A script flourish ("Cookies") appears only on the cookie card. Use script at most once, as an ornament.
- Numbers (phone, prices) are set in the display serif at headline size, never in the sans.

## Color usage
- About 40% cream `#f3f3ec`, 35% photography (saturated sky blue, white stucco, pool turquoise, magenta bougainvillea), 15% aubergine `#340c24` (footer, deep blocks), 10% navy text `#17233b`.
- Powder blue `#b5cedb` is used only for the big arch. Blush `#f8bbcb` appears as a soft tint echoing the bougainvillea.
- Text is navy on cream and cream/white on photos and aubergine. There is no pure black anywhere.

## Imagery & iconography
- Architectural renders and lifestyle photography: white Mediterranean villas, terraces covered in bougainvillea, palm trees, pools at blue hour, and linen-toned interiors. Photos are always full-bleed or large, with 0 radius.
- Cut-out bougainvillea branches overlap section edges (top-right corners) as a decorative layer.
- The only mark is the 8-petal flower. Other icons are small chevrons `‹ ›` in carousels and hotspot rings on photos. There is no icon set.

## Signature details
1. **Ultra-condensed Didone capitals** at 90–173px for single words and the phone number, centred under a 1px vertical line and a 10px extended-caps kicker.
2. **The arch**: a giant semicircle-topped powder-blue panel rising over a photograph, with text set along its curve.
3. **Circular elements**: the ring-text badge logo, and outline circle CTAs (~90px, 1px white stroke, "BOOK A CALL NOW" in 8px caps) floating on photos.
4. **Left scroll rail**: vertical `SCROLL` label, 1px line and a `00` counter fixed at the left edge.
5. **Aubergine footer** with the phone number as the headline.

## Interaction & motion
- Slow, soft easing `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` at 0.4–1.2s. The arch grows from the bottom as you scroll. Photos zoom slightly (1.1 → 1.0). Labels fade and lift 10–20px.
- Hover: circle CTAs fill/scale by about 5%. Links underline from the left. Carousels move with arrows and a thin progress line.

## Anti-patterns
- No rounded cards, pill buttons or UI chrome. Shapes are rectangles, full circles and the arch only.
- No bold grotesk headlines and no lowercase display headlines. The serif is always caps and condensed.
- No dark mode and no black. Deep areas are aubergine.
- No dense grids of cards. One image or one statement per viewport.
- No stock icons, emoji or gradient buttons.
