Source: https://thelinestudio.com/ (The Line, an animation studio in East London working on advertising, music videos (Gorillaz) and original content; Awwwards Site of the Month, Nov 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- An animation studio's reel turned into a print zine. Signal-red fields, a chunky cut-out "THE LINE®" wordmark, **tilted cards** sliding over each other like layout sheets on a desk, and animator's overscan/crop marks.
- The audience is agencies, brands and music labels. The one idea is **"the line"**: craft of drawing and framing, shown through slanted frames, crop guides and bold graphic red.

## Composition & layout
- **Top bar (tiny, 9px caps):** the "THE LINE" logo left, "● CLOSED (10–6PM)" status and "LONDON, ENGLAND", and at right a **slash-separated nav** "● HOME / WORK / ENTERTAINMENT / ABOUT / FEED / PODCAST / CONTACT / SHOP" with the current item in red with a dot.
- **Hero:** a full red `#dd0000` field with the giant blocky wordmark **"THE LINE"** (heavy, slab-cut letters with a strike-through line) along the bottom edge, "®" circled at the top-right of it, and a tiny "SCROLL TO EXPLORE".
- **Highlights:** a big section title ("Highlights", ~175px, weight 500, tracking −0.04em) on an off-white card **rotated ~−3°** over a grey `#dddee2` ground. Project cards slide in rotated (±3–5°) with the project name ("Marvel Snap / Hero") at ~40px and **overscan frame guides** (dotted rectangles, "16:9", "CROP", "ACTION SAFE" labels) drawn over the media.
- **The Studio:** title left, a small blurb right ("We're a globally renowned animation studio based in East LDN…"), photo cards tilted, a pull-quote "BELLISSIMO" from a press list, and a clients line as a big slash-separated list with logos inline ("Gucci / Riot Games / Mercedes-Benz / Marvel / Virgin / Gorillaz / Garena").
- **Reel:** a black band with "▶ Reel" centred.
- **News:** 2-column cards with image, tiny date meta, and caps headline.
- **Footer:** a full red field, the black wordmark tilted across the top on an off-white band, a duotone red anime illustration, 4 small columns (reach out / find us / social / nav), "Newsletter →" big at bottom-left, and credits in tiny caps.
- "Let's Talk →" at ~100px closes the page.

## Typography voice
- One grotesk (Denim; substitute Inter Tight) at 400–500. Section titles are huge (96–175px), tightly tracked (−0.04em), sentence case, and weight 500 (not bold).
- Micro text is 9–10px caps with slashes as separators (`/ MAKE YOUR MARK`, `/ THE LOWDOWN`).
- The wordmark is a custom chunky block logotype with a horizontal line cut through it. Do not substitute it with a font for headings.

## Color usage
- About 40% off-white `#f8f8f8`, 25% signal reds (`#dd0000` / `#ff391e`), 20% black `#0b0b0b`, 15% light grey `#dddee2`.
- Red is used as whole fields (hero, footer, card grounds), never as small text links (except the active nav item).
- Illustrations in the footer are duotone red/black.

## Imagery & iconography
- Animation stills (anime-style characters, jungle scenes, product animation), campaign photography and 2D illustration, in rectangular cards that are often rotated.
- Technical frame overlays: dotted overscan rectangles with tiny labels, like a storyboard or animation layout sheet.
- Icons: → arrows, ▶ play, ● status dots and the ® mark.

## Signature details
1. **Chunky block "THE LINE®" wordmark** with a cut line, on a red field.
2. **Tilted cards** (±3–5°) stacking and sliding as you scroll.
3. **Overscan / crop-mark overlays** on media (16:9, ACTION SAFE).
4. **Slash-separated nav and client lists** with an active red dot.
5. **Red footer** with a tilted wordmark band and a duotone illustration.

## Interaction & motion
- Expo-out easing (`cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.22, 1)`, ~0.6s). Cards rotate and translate into place on scroll. Titles slide up. The wordmark tilts on scroll into the footer.
- Hovering a project reveals "View project" and plays the clip. Nav items get the red dot on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No rounded corners, pill buttons or soft shadows.
- No bold heavy headlines in the grotesk (weight 500 max). Heaviness belongs to the wordmark.
- No multicolour palette. Red, black, off-white and grey only.
- Don't align everything. Rotated cards and slanted bands are core.
