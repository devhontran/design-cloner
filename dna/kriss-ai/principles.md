Source: https://kriss.ai/ (Kriss.ai, an AI chatbot for dental clinics; Awwwards Site of the Month, Oct 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A pastel clay-render dollhouse of a dental clinic that the camera tours room by room. Each room (Front Desk, Patient Consultation, Treatment & Surgery, Doctor's Office, Administration, Post-Operative Care) explains one thing the AI assistant does.
- The audience is dentists and clinic owners. The one idea is **"your whole practice, assisted"**: a friendly, tangible 3D building replaces abstract AI imagery, and the UI floats on frosted glass.

## Composition & layout
- **Hero:** a dusty-pink gradient sky with soft 3D clouds. A centred typewriter headline "Hi, I'm Kriss." (~48px bold, blinking cursor) and a sub-line "An AI chatbot for dentists", with a small "Learn More" button framed by **corner brackets**. The isometric clinic building sits below and the camera flies into it on scroll.
- **Room tour:** the camera pans between rooms. For each room, a **frosted glass info card** at left (~200px wide) shows the room name (~24px), a short paragraph, "Kriss.ai assists with:", 3 icon rows separated by hairlines, and a coloured full-width "Explore Features →" bar matching the room's colour (coral, mint, lilac, sand, blue).
- The nav is a frosted pill bar top-right (Home · Setup · Plans · About · FAQ) with a "Book Demo" button whose colour **changes per room**. The "KRISS AI" letterspaced wordmark sits top-left.
- A tour control bottom-right (grid icon ◻, ← →) and a chat launcher "K" square. A speech bubble "I will live down here on your website. Click to try me!" introduces the chatbot.
- **Corner-bracket framing** (L-shaped strokes at 2–4 corners) around the nav, CTAs and cards is a recurring motif.

## Typography voice
- One geometric sans (custom "Krissai"; substitute Manrope). Headlines are bold, sentence case and friendly. Card titles are medium ~24px.
- Body is small (10–12px), and list items 10px with tiny colourful emoji-like icons.
- The wordmark is spaced caps "K R I S S" with a boxed "AI".

## Color usage
- About 45% dusty pink/rose `#e9dfe1` (sky and walls), 35% pastel room colours (lilac `#cbaed9`, mint `#afd9ae` / `#74be91`, peach, powder blue, butter), 15% frosted white glass, 5% near-black text and buttons.
- Each room owns one pastel, which tints its card CTA and the nav's Book Demo button.
- Rare deep accents (forest green `#1a3d1c`, oxblood `#6c0000`) appear in furniture only.

## Imagery & iconography
- Soft clay-style 3D renders: isometric cutaway building, rounded furniture, stylised people, dental chairs and plants, with soft ambient-occlusion shadows and no hard edges.
- Tiny colourful icons (heart, calendar, shield, chat) in list rows. Hotspot "+" circles on the 3D scene.

## Signature details
1. **Isometric 3D clinic dollhouse** toured by a scroll-driven camera.
2. **Frosted glass room cards** with icon list rows and a colour-coded Explore bar.
3. **Corner-bracket frames** around UI elements.
4. **Per-room colour theming** that also recolours the nav CTA.
5. **Typewriter "Hi, I'm Kriss."** hero with 3D clouds and a chatbot speech bubble.

## Interaction & motion
- Smooth camera moves between rooms (≈1s ease-in-out). Cards fade and slide in with blur. Hotspots pulse.
- Tour controls allow ← → stepping, and the typewriter cycles phrases ("I'm integrated into…").
- Buttons shift colour on room change (≈0.3s).

## Anti-patterns
- No dark mode, black backgrounds or neon colours.
- No flat stock illustrations or photography. Use soft 3D renders only.
- No heavy borders or shadows on cards. Use frosted glass and bracket corners.
- No dense text. Each room gets one short paragraph and three bullets.
