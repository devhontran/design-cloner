Source: https://iventions.com/ (Iventions, an international events, exhibitions and congress agency based in Barcelona/London; designed by Huy Phan with Serious Business; Awwwards Site of the Day, Nov 4, 2025 + Developer Award). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A stage lit by two spotlights: periwinkle `#8072ff`/`#9c93e8` and lime `#e0ff98` cut the warm off-white page into **big angled planes** (like light beams), with a massive "IVENTIONS" wordmark half in the light and half in the shadow.
- The audience is corporates, sports federations (UEFA) and pharma. The one idea is **"step into the spotlight"**: energetic but premium event production, bold colour planes and warm editorial serif copy.

## Composition & layout
- **Hero:** off-white `#f3efeb` with huge diagonal periwinkle polygons (rounded corners) crossing the frame. "Step into / the Spotlight" (~24px medium) at left, a small grey 3-line blurb at right, and the giant wordmark "IVENTIONS" (~200px, heavy) at the bottom, coloured **periwinkle where it overlaps the beam and beige elsewhere**.
- **Chrome:** a lime pill "MENU" top-left, the "IVENTIONS" logo centred, a lime pill "GOT A PROJECT?" top-right, all tiny caps.
- **Statement:** a lime field with a giant centred sentence ("Designing experiences that audiences feel, not just attend.", ~64px).
- **Featured case:** a full-bleed event photo with the title overlaid in white (~36px), a white pill "VIEW OUR WORK", and meta words (Sports · Budapest) in serif.
- **Clients:** a black `#1e1e1e` panel. "We are proud to have worked with" in small serif at left, and a big stacked client list in white (~36px), with the active item in lime and its logo shown at right in lime.
- **Services (01 Events / 02 Exhibits / 03 Congresses):** each a pastel field (lime-cream, pale cyan, lavender). Title top-left (~64px), a big number "01" (~64px), a **serif** sub-list (~20px, "Global Events, Brand Activations, Experience Content"), a small grey paragraph, a rounded photo at right, and a "See what we create →" link with a periwinkle underline progress bar.
- **Stats:** a label at left ("Projects Delivered", "Lightbulb Moments"), a **giant lime numeral overlaid on a tilted photo card** ("270+", "1.2K") in the middle, and a small serif note at right.
- **Cities:** a periwinkle field with a stacked list of cities in lime (~64px: Amsterdam, Vienna, Lisbon, Paris, Barcelona, Munich).
- **Footer:** black, "Step into the Spotlight" (~36px), serif link columns, and the giant wordmark in lime **fading/gradient** at the bottom edge.

## Typography voice
- **Söhne** (substitute Inter) at 500–700 for display and headings: tight tracking (−0.03 to −0.04em), sentence case for statements, heavy caps for the wordmark.
- **ABC Arizona Mix** serif (substitute Newsreader, light) for secondary lists, quotes, meta words, footer links and testimonial quotes. Warm and editorial.
- Tiny caps (8–9px) for pills and meta labels.

## Color usage
- About 35% off-white `#f3efeb`, 20% periwinkle (`#8072ff` / `#9c93e8`), 15% lime `#e0ff98`, 20% near-black `#1e1e1e`, 10% event photography (magenta/blue stage lighting).
- Lime is the CTA and highlight colour (pills, active list items, numerals, city names). Periwinkle is the beam/plane colour.
- Section tints: lime-cream `#f4ffd6`, pale cyan, lavender `#bfb8ff`, peach `#ffddc4`.

## Imagery & iconography
- Event photography with stage lighting, crowds, exhibits and venues. Rounded corners (~12px) in cards, and full-bleed in features.
- Angled polygons with softly rounded corners as the main graphic motif. Small → arrows in square buttons.

## Signature details
1. **Diagonal spotlight planes** in periwinkle cutting across off-white, with the giant wordmark split in two colours by the beam.
2. **Lime pill chrome** ("MENU", "GOT A PROJECT?").
3. **Söhne + Arizona serif pairing**: grotesk headlines with serif lists and quotes.
4. **Giant lime numerals over tilted photo cards** for stats.
5. **Stacked big-type lists** (clients on black, cities on periwinkle) with a lime active state.

## Interaction & motion
- `cubic-bezier(0.165, 0.84, 0.44, 1)` at ~0.6s. Beams rotate and skew with scroll. The wordmark slides. Lists highlight on scroll position. Numerals count up.
- Service sections have an underline progress bar. Pills lift slightly on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No cold greys or pure white backgrounds. Base is warm off-white.
- No gradients except the footer wordmark fade. Colour is flat planes.
- No serif headlines. The serif stays secondary.
- Don't use periwinkle and lime as small accents only. They must appear as big fields or planes.
