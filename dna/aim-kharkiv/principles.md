Source: https://aim.obys.agency/ (AIM — AI Modernism of Kharkiv, an AI art experiment by Obys Agency reimagining 1910–1930 Kharkiv avant-garde; Awwwards Site of the Month, Jan 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It reads like a Swiss/constructivist exhibition catalogue printed on bone paper. There is a monumental black block wordmark, a strict hairline grid of tiny captions, and AI-generated images of avant-garde fashion, architecture and furniture in vermilion, cobalt and cream.
- The audience is designers and art lovers. The one idea is **"modernism rebuilt by machine"**: rigid typographic order framing lush, painterly AI imagery.

## Composition & layout
- **Hero:** the wordmark "AIM" built from heavy geometric black blocks (slanted A, bar I, chevron M) spans the full width at the top, with a full-width black bar under it. Below is a 4-column hairline grid: a meta row of tiny captions (site name · Index / Experiment / About · [Gallery] · Obys Agency ©2025), a left cell listing "Modernists: E01 … E05" names, a vertical 1px divider, and a title block "AIM— / AI Modernism / Of Kharkiv" (~48px) bottom-left of column 2. "Scroll to Explore ↓" sits bottom-right.
- **Manifesto:** one long paragraph at ~40px, tight leading, spanning the full width, with footnote markers `[*]` and animated black rectangles (pixel blocks) moving across the text. Footnotes sit in a 3-column row: `[*] — Collective name …`, `[**] — Main visual AI tool is Midjourney`.
- **Gallery:** full-bleed image strips (3 images edge to edge, no gaps). Then a split layout: a big "Explore / Experiment" title (~82px) with a hairline "Learn More →" link at left, a numbered index "[06] Featured: Name: Suprematista / Bauhaus / …" (active item black, others grey) in the middle, and a tall image column at right that swaps per item. A counter "1—6" sits top.
- **Footer:** a staircase of black rectangles (descending heights) resolves into the giant "AIM" wordmark again, above a full-width black bar.
- A 1px rule and meta row stay fixed at the top of every screen.

## Typography voice
- One neo-grotesk (Khteka; substitute Inter Tight). Headlines are 500, tracking −0.03em, line-height ~0.9, sentence/title case, 40–82px.
- Captions are **10px regular**, used for everything else (nav, credits, footnotes, lists), in square-bracket notation: `[Gallery]`, `[06] Featured:`, `[*]`.
- The wordmark is custom geometry (blocks), not type.

## Color usage
- About 55% bone `#e7e4df`, 35% ink black `#141414`, 10% imagery. The UI is strictly two-tone.
- Colour lives only in AI images: vermilion `#e8542a`, cobalt blue, mustard, cream and slate, the classic constructivist palette.

## Imagery & iconography
- AI-generated (Midjourney) images: models in striped constructivist dresses, suprematist architecture with circles and stairs, Bauhaus furniture and sculptures. Full-bleed or tall columns, with no radius, border or shadow.
- Graphic elements are pure black rectangles (bars, stair steps, moving "pixels").
- Icons are only arrows (→ ↓).

## Signature details
1. **Monumental black block wordmark** spanning the full width, opening and closing the page.
2. **Hairline 4-column grid** with a fixed meta row of 10px captions.
3. **Bracket and footnote notation** (`[*]`, `[06] Featured:`, `[Gallery]`).
4. **Animated black rectangles**: bars, stairs and pixel blocks that move over text and assemble into the logo.
5. **Index list + swapping image column** for the gallery, with the active name in black and the rest grey.

## Interaction & motion
- Crisp and quick (~200ms) for hovers. Scroll drives the block animations (rectangles slide, stack and reshape into letters).
- Text blocks reveal with black rectangles wiping across them. The gallery image swaps on index hover or scroll.

## Anti-patterns
- No UI colour. Colour belongs only to imagery.
- No rounded corners, shadows or gradients.
- No mid-size body text. Type is either ≤12px captions or ≥40px statements.
- Don't break the grid. Every element aligns to the hairline columns.
