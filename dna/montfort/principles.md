Source: https://mont-fort.com/ (Montfort Group, a global commodity trading and asset investment company based in Geneva; Awwwards Site of the Month, Jun 2025). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A single continuous camera flight: you start above snowy peaks in the clouds, descend through stormy mist onto a tanker at sea, pull out to the Earth from orbit, and land in a sunlit forest. The whole corporate story plays as one breath of atmosphere.
- The audience is investors, partners and talent. The one idea is **"calm command over a global flow"**: a trading house presented through sublime nature, with quiet type and no hard edges.

## Composition & layout
- A full-viewport, continuous background film (clouds → sea → globe → forest) sits behind everything. Content blocks fade in and out over it, and the page never shows a section boundary until the white footer.
- **Hero:** the wordmark "MONTFORT" set very wide (letter-spacing ≈ 0.4em, ~34px, light) next to the geometric tree mark and a thin vertical divider, left-centre on clouds. The top-left nav is a row of tiny caps division links (MONTFORT GROUP · TRADING · CAPITAL · MARITIME · FORT ENERGY) with a short 1px underline on the active one. Top-right has "NEWS" with a count dot and "MENU".
- Statements are 4–5 lines of **light caps** (~40–50px, tracking +0.045em, line-height 1.4), placed left or right third and never centred.
- Division blocks show a **diamond outline marker** with a number (◇1, ◇2…), a small grey title ("Montfort Trading"), a 2–3 line caps statement, and an underlined caps link with a round white arrow button ("MONTFORT MARITIME ⊙→").
- A thin **circle outline** (~24px) follows as a scroll/cursor indicator. The bottom-right has ↑ and — scroll glyphs, and the bottom-left reads "SCROLL DOWN TO DISCOVER".
- Dense info (ethics, ESG) appears as 3-column small text over dark navy, and ESG pillars are drawn as circle outline icons with captions.
- **Footer:** plain white with link lists in tiny caps, 3 office columns (Geneva, Dubai, Singapore) with caps city names, and the wordmark repeated at the bottom.

## Typography voice
- A geometric sans (Century Gothic; substitute Josefin Sans / Mulish), **light 300–400 and UPPERCASE with open tracking** for statements. It is calm and airy, and never bold.
- Body is 14–16px sentence case, line-height 1.6, with generous tracking (~0.02em).
- Micro labels are 8–10px caps, tracking ~0.2em (nav, "WHO WE ARE", link labels).

## Color usage
- A steel-blue atmosphere: mist `#e5ecf1`, steel `#81a0bb`, deep blue `#2d628c` for text on light, and navy-black `#0f1d2a` in storm and space scenes. The forest adds muted greens late in the journey.
- Text is deep blue on light mist and white on dark scenes. There is no accent colour; hierarchy comes from light and scale.
- White is reserved for the footer and small round arrow buttons.

## Imagery & iconography
- Cinematic, desaturated nature and industry footage (mountains, clouds, tankers, Earth from space, forest light rays), always full-bleed and blended with fog so scenes melt into each other.
- Icons are thin 1px outlines: diamonds with numbers, circles, small arrows inside white discs, and circle-framed ESG pictograms.
- The logo is a geometric tree made of small squares.

## Signature details
1. **One continuous atmospheric fly-through** (clouds → sea → orbit → forest) as the page background.
2. **Light, wide-tracked geometric caps** for all statements, with the very wide "M O N T F O R T" wordmark.
3. **Diamond-number markers** (◇1–◇4) for divisions, plus thin circle cursor/scroll indicators.
4. **Underlined caps links with round white arrow buttons.**
5. **No section edges**: content fades over the film until a plain white footer.

## Interaction & motion
- Slow and cinematic: long cross-fades (~1–1.5s, `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)`). Scroll scrubs the camera through the film. Text rises and fades line by line.
- The circle indicator follows the cursor and grows over links. Arrow buttons slide their arrow on hover.
- Respect reduced motion by swapping the film for static stills per chapter.

## Anti-patterns
- No bold or heavy type and no lowercase display statements.
- No cards, boxes, shadows or rounded panels over the film. Content floats directly on atmosphere.
- No saturated colour or brand accent. Keep the blue-grey mist palette.
- No hard section cuts or stripes of solid colour before the footer.
