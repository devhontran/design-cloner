Source: https://mindmarket.com/ (MindMarket, a global qualitative market-research partner based in Dubai and London; Awwwards Site of the Month, Dec 2025). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It reads like a joyful picture book for a serious B2B service. Huge flat-colour illustrations of exaggerated, stretchy people (big shoes, long limbs, binoculars, trumpets) run through the page, while white cards calmly explain the service.
- The audience is brand, product and insight teams. The one idea is **"real human insights"**: people-first, global and friendly, with human warmth expressed through illustration and bouncy motion.

## Composition & layout
- A **floating white nav bar** (rounded 10px, ~40px tall) inset from the edges holds the logo, text links with ⌄ dropdowns, a round lime menu button, and a separate white "Get a quote" button with a round blue avatar icon.
- **Hero:** a full lime `#8ed462` field with a centred headline (~140px, 2 lines, weight 500, tracking −0.06em), a small sub-line, and an illustration of characters leaping out of the bottom edge.
- **Story scroll:** a single long illustration (a winding lime "road" on cream `#f5f1e4`) that characters travel along. **White cards** (rounded ~10px, ~330px wide) hold a 30px headline ("One brief. One team.", "Global, for real."), a paragraph and a coloured button. They are pinned alternately left and right over the art.
- **Stats:** a big 3-line headline left with a **squiggle underline** under one word, and a stack of **tilted rounded number cards** (white "60+", lime "400", coral "50+") that shuffle as you scroll.
- **Clients:** a grey `#e0dbce` rounded panel (~64px radius) with rows of white pill logos (icon + name) drifting horizontally.
- **Footer:** a full yellow `#f5e211` panel with rounded top corners, a giant "Let's / Connect" (~140px) with a lime squiggle underline, link columns, and office addresses.

## Typography voice
- **Inter only**, weight 500 everywhere (headlines and body). Headlines use very tight tracking (−0.06em) and line-height 0.95, sentence case, and short punchy phrases with a full stop ("One brief. One team.").
- Body is 15–20px, weight 500, line-height 1.25, in short friendly paragraphs.
- Emphasis comes from a hand-drawn **wavy squiggle underline** in lilac or lime under a key word. No italics or bold.

## Color usage
- About 35% cream `#f5f1e4`, 20% lime `#8ed462`, 15% white cards, 15% illustration colours, 10% yellow `#f5e211` (footer), 5% ink `#2c2e2a` text.
- The illustration palette is vivid flat primaries: coral `#ff705d`, sky blue `#2ba0ff`, sunflower yellow, lilac, lime and white. Each button takes one of these colours to match its section (coral "Services", blue "Industry Sectors", lilac "Our Network").
- Text is always ink `#2c2e2a`, never pure black.

## Imagery & iconography
- Flat vector character illustrations with no outlines and big simple shapes: rubber-hose limbs, giant sneakers, tiny heads with sunglasses, confetti squiggles and clouds. They are huge, often larger than the viewport, and bleed off edges.
- Real photography appears only in the insights/blog area (rounded 12px, candid research sessions) and one hero photo wrapped by a yellow 3D ribbon with curved text.
- Button icons are small round badges with a chevron `›` inside, in a contrasting colour.

## Signature details
1. **Giant flat character illustrations** that the page scrolls through like a path.
2. **White rounded cards over the art** holding a headline, paragraph and colour-matched button.
3. **Button = label + round coloured chevron badge** ("Get a quote ⦿›").
4. **Wavy squiggle underlines** on key words in headlines.
5. **Big rounded colour blocks** (lime hero, grey client panel, yellow footer with 64px corners) and **tilted stat cards**.

## Interaction & motion
- Bouncy and elastic: `cubic-bezier(0.17, 0.67, 0.3, 1.33)` (overshoot) at ~0.4s for hovers and card reveals. Characters move and parallax along the scroll path.
- Stat cards shuffle and rotate as they stack. Client pills drift in rows.
- Buttons nudge their chevron badge on hover, and nav dropdowns open with a slight bounce.

## Anti-patterns
- No dark mode and no black backgrounds.
- No thin/light or heavy/black font weights. Keep 500 throughout.
- No sharp corners on cards or panels, and no stock-photo heroes. Illustration leads.
- No gradients or glass effects. Colours are flat.
- No serious corporate iconography. Keep it human and playful.
