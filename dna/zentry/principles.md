Source: https://zentry.com/ (Zentry, a gaming / AI "Human-Agentic OS" platform; Awwwards Site of the Month, Aug 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend. Note: the site has been updated since the award (copy now centres on "Human-Agentic OS"). The captures here show the current version, and scroll capture stalled on the preloader, so lower sections are described from extracted styles and page text, not screenshots.

## Essence
- A game-launch trailer crossed with a crypto manifesto. Screaming ultra-condensed caps are split across the screen ("REDEFINE … REALITY") around a glowing particle-mesh flower, then everything turns to calm lavender pages with mono text.
- The audience is gamers, crypto and AI communities, and investors. The one idea is **"a new reality"**: loud, cinematic display type against quiet technical copy.

## Composition & layout
- **Preloader:** a violet `#5542ff` "Z" bolt mark centred on black.
- **Hero:** black full screen with a **real-time glowing mesh/particle object** (violet-pink-cyan iridescent wireframe flower) centred. The headline is **split to opposite corners**: "REDEFINE" top-left and "REALITY" bottom-right (~160px condensed caps in lavender `#dfdff2`). A 4-line mono paragraph sits at left ("Enter the Human-Agentic OS / The substrate where …").
- **Nav:** "ZENTRY" wordmark left with two small pills ("PRODUCTS ▾", "WHITEPAPER"); right, small caps links with ▾ (zTERMINAL, ABOUT, CONTACT).
- **Content sections** sit on lavender `#dfdff2`: giant condensed headlines where **some letters are set bolder or in a different style** inside words, a tiny caps kicker ("Welcome to Zentry", "who we are", "OUR KEY METRICS"), and mono paragraphs of max ~40ch.
- Product cards (DATA, TERMINAL, RADIANT, ZIGMA) are large rounded panels (~12–24px) with video backgrounds, a condensed name top-left, a mono description, and a small pill CTA. Cards tilt in 3D toward the cursor.
- Media reveals use a **clip-path polygon** that expands from a skewed quad to full frame as you scroll.
- Backer/partner logos sit in simple monochrome rows.

## Typography voice
- **Ultra-condensed heavy display caps** (Zentry Screamer; substitute Anton or Big Shoulders Display 900), 100–200px, line-height ~0.82. Headlines are broken word by word across lines.
- **Roboto Mono** for all body, labels and UI (10–16px), sentence case for paragraphs and caps for small labels.
- Emphasis inside a headline swaps individual letters to a bolder or alternate style, not colour.

## Color usage
- About 45% lavender `#dfdff2` (content pages), 35% black (hero, dark sections), 10% electric violet `#5542ff` (logo, 3D glow, active states), 5% acid yellow `#edff66` (CTA pills and highlights), 5% greys.
- Yellow is reserved for primary pill CTAs, and violet for brand and glow.

## Imagery & iconography
- Real-time 3D: an iridescent particle/wireframe organic object with bloom. Game and product footage inside rounded cards.
- Icons are small ▾ carets and ↗ arrows. The logo is the violet Z bolt.

## Signature details
1. **Split giant condensed headline** across opposite corners around a central 3D object.
2. **Glowing particle-mesh 3D hero** on black.
3. **Lavender content pages with mono body copy** under screaming caps.
4. **Clip-path skewed-quad video reveals** and 3D-tilting product cards.
5. **Acid-yellow pill CTAs** with a violet brand mark.

## Interaction & motion
- Scroll-driven reveals (0.5–1s ease). Clip-path frames open, headlines slide in word by word, and cards tilt with the cursor (perspective ~1000px).
- The 3D object rotates and reacts to the cursor. Pills invert on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No regular-width sans headlines and no serif fonts.
- No pure white page backgrounds. Content pages are lavender.
- No colourful gradients in UI. Colour glow belongs to 3D and video.
- No long proportional body text. Keep paragraphs short and mono.
