Source: https://akaru.fr/ (Akaru, a web design and development agency in Lyon; Awwwards Site of the Month, Apr 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A confident French agency folio that scrolls **sideways** through pastel colour slabs. Crisp photography sits in tall panels, a light grotesk carries the voice, and a towering ultra-condensed "AKARU" wordmark opens and closes the story.
- The audience is brands looking for a web partner. The one idea is **"precision with lightness"**: strict typographic calm, playful colour blocking and craft details like dot-pills, index rules and an awards ledger.

## Composition & layout
- **Horizontal scroll** (the page moves left as you scroll down). Sections are tall vertical panels of different widths placed side by side: an off-white intro panel, full-height photo panels, and narrow **pastel colour slabs** (powder blue `#bfccd8`, mauve `#b692a1`, sage `#798e7b`, apricot `#e49366`).
- **Intro panel:** a small top nav with coloured bullet dots (● Projets ● Expertises ● Agence ● Contact), the giant **ultra-condensed "AKARU"** wordmark (~160px tall, black) with a 2-line agency blurb under it, and social links over a 1px rule at the bottom.
- **Project cards:** tall photo on top, and a colour footer panel below with year pill ("2025"), category and description in tiny caps, a big project name (~48px light), and a round outline `↗` button.
- **Section heads:** "Projets", "Expertises", "Agence", "Nos prix" at ~64–96px, weight 400, tight tracking, top-left of the panel. They have a numbered index list at right (00, 01, 02 … with 1px rules and tiny caps labels right-aligned) and a large statement paragraph (~24px) at the bottom.
- **Awards ledger:** a black panel with a table of rows (SITE OF THE DAY · project name · ↗), 1px white rules, and one row highlighted in white. A 3D rotating trophy object sits beside it.
- **Footer:** black, with contact columns in tiny caps and the giant white "AKARU" wordmark cropped at the bottom edge. A "NOUS CONTACTER ●" outline pill sits at top.
- A **floating black pill "MENU ●"** (with an orange dot) stays top-right.

## Typography voice
- **Alliance Neue** (substitute Inter) at 400–500 for headlines and statements: sentence case, tight tracking, calm.
- **Ultra-condensed heavy display** for the wordmark only (custom; substitute Big Shoulders Display 900 or Anton), always the brand name, huge.
- Micro text in 8–10px caps with wide tracking (labels, list items "/ IDENTITÉ VISUELLE / LOGO", table rows).
- French copy, with slash-prefixed tag lists.

## Color usage
- About 35% off-white `#f1efeb`, 25% black `#0e0e0e`, 25% photography, 15% pastel slabs (blue-grey, mauve, sage, apricot). Violet `#7345ff` and apricot appear in project imagery and small dots.
- Pastels are used as **flat full-height slabs** between content panels, never as small accents.

## Imagery & iconography
- High-quality campaign photography (a white cat, sneakers, portraits, alpine resort, architecture) cropped into tall panels with no radius.
- A 3D object (awards trophy / wordmark block) rotates on dark panels.
- Icons: a `↗` inside thin circles, and coloured dots in nav and pills.

## Signature details
1. **Horizontal-scrolling panel strip** with full-height pastel colour slabs between content.
2. **Ultra-condensed giant AKARU wordmark** at the start and cropped in the footer.
3. **Pill buttons with a dot** ("MENU ●", "VOIR LES PROJETS ●", "NOUS CONTACTER ●").
4. **Numbered index lists** with right-aligned tiny caps labels over 1px rules.
5. **Awards ledger table** on black with one inverted row and a rotating 3D trophy.

## Interaction & motion
- Vertical scroll is mapped to horizontal travel with smooth inertia (`cubic-bezier(0.455, 0.03, 0.515, 0.955)`). Panels parallax at different speeds.
- Photos scale slightly on hover, and the `↗` circle fills. Index rows extend their rule and reveal a preview.
- The footer wordmark letters rise from the bottom like columns.

## Anti-patterns
- No gradients, shadows or glass. Blocks are flat.
- No bold headlines in the grotesk. Weight 400–500 only.
- No square buttons. Use pills (or circles for icons).
- Don't use the condensed display for anything but the brand name.
