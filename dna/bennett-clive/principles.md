Source: https://bennettandclive.com (Bennett & Clive, a global production company for fashion, beauty and advertising; Awwwards Site of the Month, Mar 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A client list turned into a typographic poster. Giant, ultra-tight black and white Inter caps stack brand names (ADIDAS, CALVIN KLEIN, HOURGLASS, NIKE, EILISH, MAC), and hovering or scrolling reveals the production work behind them as video.
- The audience is brands, agencies and photographers. The one idea is **"the names speak"**: pure typographic confidence with zero decoration, where the work appears only when invited.

## Composition & layout
- The nav is a tiny row top-right (HOME · WORK · ABOUT · CONTACT, 11px bold caps, active item underlined). A thin mid-screen line of labels runs across: "BENNETT" at left, "&" at centre, "CLIVE" at right, as a split wordmark.
- **Work index:** a black full-viewport section with client/project names in **~150px bold caps, line-height 0.77, tracking −0.07em**, stacked **right-aligned** so the ragged left edge forms a shape. The list scrolls through the frame, and the active name reveals its video/photo full-bleed behind the type.
- **About:** white section. The statement "Let's / raise / your" (~64px, lowercase-ish sentence case, very tight) is left-aligned, and the services list ("Global Production / Line Production / Shoot Architecture / Casting & Talent Management / Art Buying / Motion / Post Production / Editorial & Finishing", ~34px bold) is right-aligned. A tiny outline pill "About" sits above.
- **Clients cloud:** a centred block mixing bold names (Sephora, Hourglass, Stella McCartney) with each brand's own logotype in its native style (PRADA, ZARA, DIOR, VOGUE, CLINIQUE, GRAFF), justified into a tight typographic paragraph.
- **Footer:** 4 small columns (city clocks "New York 01:35 / Los Angeles 22:35 / Miami 01:35", contact, socials, legal, 10px bold) and the wordmark "BENNETT & CLIVE" **edge to edge** at ~130px along the bottom.

## Typography voice
- **Inter only**, heavy (700–800) for everything visible. Display caps are extremely tight (−0.07em) with line-height below 0.8, so lines nearly touch.
- UI and footer are 10–11px bold. There is no light weight and no italics.
- Client logos are allowed in their own typefaces inside the name cloud, the only exception.

## Color usage
- Strictly black `#000000` and white `#ffffff`: black work sections and white about/footer. Colour appears only in revealed photography and video.

## Imagery & iconography
- Fashion and beauty campaign stills and videos (from productions they've done), revealed full-bleed behind or beside names on hover or scroll, with no frames.
- No icons except underlines. The only shape is the tiny outline pill label.

## Signature details
1. **Giant ultra-tight Inter caps name stacks**, right-aligned on black.
2. **Split wordmark rail**: BENNETT · & · CLIVE spread across the viewport at mid-height.
3. **Logo-word mashup client cloud** mixing bold Inter names with real brand logotypes.
4. **Edge-to-edge wordmark footer** with live city clocks.
5. **Hover-reveal media** behind typography, with no thumbnails grid.

## Interaction & motion
- Names slide and scroll vertically with inertia (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.94, 0.6, 1)`, ~0.8s), and the active name swaps the background video.
- Services and statements reveal line by line. Clocks tick. Nav underline moves on hover.

## Anti-patterns
- No colour in UI, no greys for headings, and no gradients.
- No thumbnails grid, cards, rounded corners or shadows.
- No loose tracking or line-height on display type. It must feel dense and tight.
- No light or thin weights, and no serif fonts.
