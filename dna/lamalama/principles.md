Source: https://lamalama.com/ (Lama Lama, a creative digital agency in Amsterdam; Awwwards Site of the Month, Jul 2026). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It reads like a confident studio's control panel: graphite surfaces, cream type, a dithered halftone texture and small mono "system" labels everywhere. The copy is blunt and funny ("All in or not at all", "Make the first move, we won't tell").
- The audience is brands looking for a digital agency. The one idea is **a tight crew with personality in the details**: a strict grid and heavy headlines, with jokes hidden in the micro-UI (rotating nav taglines, pixel smiley logo, live clock).

## Composition & layout
- 40px outer margins on a 12-column grid. The hero headline is bottom-left (3 lines, ~72px) with an intro paragraph bottom-right (cols 9–12). A full-width 1px rule sits above the footer strip.
- The **floating centre nav** is a dark block (~438×50px) holding the pixel logo left, a rotating micro tagline centred ("LOOKING SHARP TODAY", "OUR GREATEST HITS", "BLAME THESE PEOPLE") and a hamburger right. It changes text per section.
- A **floating right-side widget stack** (≈160px wide) holds "GET IN TOUCH" with avatar (light grey card) and accordion cards "THIS IS US ( + )", "PITCHDECK ( + )", "OUR AWWWARDS TALK ( + )". They expand in place to show video or image previews.
- Statement paragraphs are ~28px regular, max ~30ch, with the **first line indented** by ~2 words. Section heads are ALL-CAPS heavy with a full stop ("WHAT WE DO.", "BRIEF US SOMETHING.").
- The work list is full-width rows ~84px tall separated by 1px rules. Each row has the project name (14px) at left, tiny tag chips, a `( + )` toggle, and a strip of 4–6 thumbnails at right (grey placeholders that load and reveal on hover).
- Services are 3 mono columns ([ BRANDING ] [ DIGITAL ] [ MARKETING ]) of 12px lists. The client logo marquee is monochrome.
- A **footer status strip** repeats on the hero and the end: `20+ DIGITAL FREAKS · AMSTERDAM BASED · [ ● 04 : 22 : 33 ] · FOLLOW US · INSTAGRAM + · LINKEDIN + · NL ◐ EN`.

## Typography voice
- Suisse Int'l (substitute Inter) at two weights. Headlines are **700, UPPERCASE, line-height 0.8–0.9, tracking −0.02em**, 56–80px, always ending with a period. Body and statements are 400 sentence case, 16–28px.
- Micro-UI uses a monospace (Sometype Mono), 10–12px UPPERCASE, wrapped in **square brackets** or parentheses: `[ WE ARE LAMA LAMA ]`, `[ GET IN TOUCH ]`, `( + )`, `( VIEW + )`.
- Buttons are mono caps + `↗`.

## Color usage
- About 60% graphite `#1a1c1c`, 20% cream text `#f9f4eb`, 10% surface greys `#232525`/`#303231`, 10% imagery.
- **Red `#ff0000` is a signal, not a brand fill**: it is the live-clock dot and appears in project imagery. It is never used for buttons or headings.
- Light grey (≈`#bdbab4`) is used only on the "GET IN TOUCH" card, the single light element in the UI.

## Imagery & iconography
- Photography is candid studio life (people at screens, plants, workshop) and project mock-ups on bold colours. Images are rectangular with 0–2px radius, in strips or large blocks.
- The logo is a pixel/8-bit mark that animates between glyphs (letters, smiley, checker).
- **Halftone dot texture**: a fine dithered dot grid over the graphite in the hero and services areas, fading out toward the content.
- Icons are `↗`, `+` and a hamburger only.

## Signature details
1. **Floating centre nav block** with a per-section rotating micro tagline in mono caps.
2. **Right-side accordion widget stack** ("GET IN TOUCH" avatar card, then `( + )` cards).
3. **Bracketed mono labels** `[ ... ]` and `( + )` toggles on everything small.
4. **Heavy caps headlines with a period**, bottom-left, line-height 0.8.
5. **Halftone dot texture** and a **live clock** `[ ● HH : MM : SS ]` in the footer strip.

## Interaction & motion
- Fast and crisp: ~200ms ease for hovers, with larger reveals using a quick stagger. Thumbnails reveal on row hover. Accordion cards expand with height and opacity.
- The nav tagline swaps per section and the pixel logo glitches between states. The clock ticks live.
- Buttons have a 1px outline that lightens on hover, and the `↗` nudges.

## Anti-patterns
- No rounded cards, pills or soft shadows. Radius is at most 2px and elevation is flat.
- No colourful UI. Red is only the live dot.
- No light theme sections. Graphite runs throughout.
- No title-case or lowercase headlines. No serif fonts.
- Don't drop the micro-UI jokes (tagline, brackets, clock). Without them it becomes a generic dark agency site.
