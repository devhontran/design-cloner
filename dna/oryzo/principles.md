Source: https://oryzo.ai (Oryzo, a tongue-in-cheek "AI-powered" cork coaster by the studio Lusion; Awwwards Site of the Month, Apr 2026). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- It is an Apple-grade product launch for something absurdly simple, a cork coaster. Deadpan copy ("Isn't just a coaster", "Powered by AI*", "AI fills the gaps. We said high five. It heard six.") meets serious 3D craft.
- The audience is designers and tech people who get the joke. The one idea is **one hero object, treated with total seriousness**: a single photoreal 3D coaster that the whole page orbits around, in warm espresso darkness.

## Composition & layout
- **Hero:** a top-down photo of a green cutting mat on a wooden desk (pencils, cutter, paper clip). The huge wordmark "ORYZO" (~160px, heavy, cream) sits top-left with a tiny kicker above ("MADE FOR MUGS. BUILT FOR TABLES."), and the 3D coaster sits centre. A frosted-glass card bottom-left ("DESIGNED BY LUSION, …" / "The world's most unnecessarily sophisticated cork coaster"), a video thumbnail bottom-right, and a vertical "ORYZO-1 MODEL" tab on the right edge.
- **After the hero** the desk fades out and the page becomes a dark stage (`#100904`) with a soft orange glow from the bottom-left corner. The coaster stays centred and **rotates and flips with scroll**.
- Three-column statement layout: a caps headline left (~29px, "ISN'T JUST / A COASTER."), the object centre, and a 4-line paragraph right (~14px).
- Big sentence-case headline centred at top ("Powered by AI*", ~72px) with a small orange product code under its end ("ORYZO-1"), plus 3D hands reaching up to hold the coaster.
- Footnotes are bottom-right in 8px caps above a dotted rule ("*ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR"). Asterisks in headlines point to them.
- Nav is a minimal top-right text row (INTRO · FEATURES · PRODUCT · CONTACT, 9px caps, active item underlined), the wordmark top-left, and a thin vertical progress bar at the right edge.

## Typography voice
- One grotesk family (Halyard Display; substitute Hanken Grotesk). The wordmark and section heads are **heavy 700 caps**, and big statements are **600 sentence case** with tight tracking (−0.02em).
- Paragraphs are 14px medium, line-height 1.3, max ~22ch. They are short, dry and funny.
- Micro text (nav, footnotes, kickers) is 8–10px caps, weight 600–700.
- Headlines often end with an asterisk `*` that links to a footnote joke.

## Color usage
- About 60% espresso black `#100904`, 20% cream `#ffedd7` (all text), 15% cork tones in the object (`#be8858`, `#9b734f`), 5% signal orange `#dc5000`.
- Orange is the product-code colour (ORYZO-1) and the ambient glow. It is never a big button or a background.
- The hero is the only colourful moment (green mat, wood desk). Everything after lives in the warm dark.

## Imagery & iconography
- One real-time 3D object (the coaster) with physically based cork material, soft shadows and rim light, plus 3D hands. The hero backdrop is photographic.
- A frosted/blurred glass card (backdrop blur, 1px light border) is the only UI surface.
- Icons are almost absent: a small circled chevron for "SCROLL TO CONTINUE" and a play thumbnail.

## Signature details
1. **One centred 3D hero object** that stays pinned and rotates, flips and scales as the page scrolls.
2. **Deadpan product copy with asterisks** and tiny footnote captions bottom-right above a dotted rule.
3. **Espresso darkness with a corner glow** (orange radial light bottom-left) instead of flat black.
4. **Blur-in headline reveals**: letters sharpen from a heavy blur as they enter.
5. **Heavy caps wordmark** top-left with a tiny caps kicker, and a vertical tab on the right edge.

## Interaction & motion
- Scroll-scrubbed 3D: rotation and position are tied to scroll progress. Text fades and blurs in (filter blur 20px → 0) over ~0.6–1s.
- Hover is subtle: nav underline slides, and the video thumbnail brightens.
- The right-edge progress bar fills with scroll.

## Anti-patterns
- No grids of cards, icons or feature lists. There is one object and one message per screen.
- No cold greys or pure black. Darks are warm brown-black.
- No bright multi-colour UI. Orange is a tiny accent only.
- Don't write earnest marketing copy. The voice is dry, self-aware understatement.
