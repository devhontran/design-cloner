Source: https://huyml.co/ (Huy Phan / HUYML, independent designer and art director in Ho Chi Minh City, Awwwards jury member with 13+ Sites of the Day; portfolio). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A designer's light-table: a soft grey page where project images **bend and curl in 3D** like printed sheets on a curved track. A column of project names scrolls in sync, and big glassy chrome numerals count the selected work.
- The audience is agencies and international clients. The one idea is **"craft, quietly shown"**: a nearly monochrome, typographically disciplined frame that lets the curved WebGL image stream carry the energy.

## Composition & layout
- A four-zone layout on light grey `#ececec`:
  1. **Far-left rail:** a vertical "HUYML©" wordmark rotated 90° with tiny meta ("copyright 2026 · hcmc, vn · +84") and a small line-art **caricature portrait** in a frame.
  2. **Left column:** "Menu" label, then a stacked nav in 20px caps ("→ WORK / ABOUT / PLAYGROUND / CONTACT", active item with an arrow). A **meta grid** for the current project sits mid-left in two tiny columns (Team/Role, Launch, Recognition lists like "Awwwards Site of the Day · CSSDA Website of the Day"). "Selected work" plus a giant counter numeral and a `/19` total sit bottom-left.
  3. **Centre:** a **vertical WebGL image carousel**. Project images are warped as if on a bending ribbon, curling and rotating as they pass, with the current one flat and centred.
  4. **Right column:** a centred list of project names in ~20px condensed caps ("MARK WOODLAND", "EISLAB", "MAT VOYCE"), each with a tiny category label above, an em-dash rule and a 3-line description. The active item is black and the others fade to ~20% grey. **Colour swatch chips** (3 tiny squares showing the project palette) sit at the far right.
- Top bar: "Audio Off —" toggle, "Working globally / HCMC, 17:52" (local clock), "For inquiries hello@huyml.co" underlined. Bottom: "Scroll" left, and "'25 showreel ▶" right.

## Typography voice
- A neutral grotesk (BT Grotesk; substitute Archivo) at 400–500 for everything. Nav and project names are caps, tightly leaded (0.9). Body and meta are 10–12px sentence case.
- Numerals are huge (~180px), rendered as **chrome / glass 3D text** reflecting the images behind (display face BT Glyphius / F37 Bolton; substitute Instrument Serif or a high-contrast display).
- Hierarchy comes from opacity (active 100%, inactive 20%), not weight.

## Color usage
- About 70% light grey `#ececec`, 15% ink `#181818`, 15% project imagery. The UI stays monochrome.
- Project colour appears only in imagery and the 3-square swatch chips. Accents like lime `#ceff45`, lilac `#c3abff` and coral `#ff5a36` come from project palettes, never UI chrome.

## Imagery & iconography
- Project photography and website screens (portraits, product shots, UI mock-ups), warped on a curved WebGL ribbon with slight chromatic highlights at the bend.
- A line-drawn self-portrait caricature is the personal mark. Icons are → arrows and ▶.

## Signature details
1. **Curved 3D image ribbon**: project images bend and curl like paper through the centre.
2. **Chrome/glass giant numerals** for the selected-work counter (`05 /19`).
3. **Synced project name list** with an opacity focus (active black, others 20% grey) plus colour swatch chips.
4. **Vertical HUYML© wordmark + caricature** on the far left.
5. **Tiny meta grid** (Team / Role / Launch / Recognition) listing awards per project.

## Interaction & motion
- Scroll drives the ribbon smoothly (~0.3s easing, damped). Images bend more at the edges and flatten in the centre, and the numeral morphs between values.
- The name list scrolls in counter-motion. Audio can be toggled. The local clock ticks.
- Hover on a name previews that project on the ribbon.

## Anti-patterns
- No colourful UI, gradients or heavy shadows. The page stays grey and ink.
- No card grids of thumbnails. Projects flow on one curved track.
- No bold headlines. Scale and opacity create hierarchy.
- Don't centre the whole layout. Keep the four-zone asymmetric grid.
