Source: https://matvoyce.tv/ (Mat Voyce, UK type designer and animator; designed by Huy Phan with Uncommon; Awwwards Site of the Day, Jan 30, 2025 + Developer Award + Typography Honors). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A type animator's toybox. Enormous italic condensed caps in electric cyan fill the screen, then the logo **morphs through different lettering styles** (bubble, chrome gradient, outline, serif, pixel) while stickers and sparkles pop around it.
- The audience is brands commissioning motion type (Amazon, Netflix, Nike, Disney+). The one idea is **"making type move"**: typography is the product, the imagery and the interaction.

## Composition & layout
- **Chrome:** the "MAT VOYCE" sticker logo top-left (black/white 3D lettering), a tiny 2-line bio at centre ("Type designer & animator, working for brands globally."), "UK based / Working globally" to its right, and "MENU +" in italic condensed caps top-right.
- **Hero:** cream/white `#fffdf8` with "MAT" (~400px, italic condensed heavy, cyan `#00d3ff`) top-left and "VOYCE" bottom-right, touching the edges. A small lime pill ticker "SCROLL —— SCROLL ——" bottom-left.
- **What he does:** a centred small label and giant italic caps in pale grey that **scroll through** ("TYPE DESIGN & ILLUSTRATION / TYPE / MOTION DESIGN"), faded like ghost text.
- **Brands:** "Brands Mat's worked with" plus a 2-row monochrome logo wall.
- **Contact:** a giant **chevron-striped lime pill** button (~480×170px, thick black outline + drop-offset shadow, animated diagonal stripes) with "CONTACT MAT" in italic caps and a tiny "Always open for new projects and collaborations".
- **Footer:** a full cyan field. The centre shows the **"MAKING TYPE MOVE" logo cycling styles** (gradient lime→blue bubble letters, outline, serif, with smileys, sparkles and a 3D "SINCE 2020" ribbon tag). Italic caps nav at left ("WORK / ABOUT / MEDIA") and socials at right ("INSTAGRAM / BEHANCE / GIPHY / LINKEDIN"). Email, "Design by Uncommon" and copyright in tiny text at the bottom.
- The custom cursor is a small 3D arrow.

## Typography voice
- Display: **italic, condensed, heavy caps** (F37 Judge; substitute Anton with faux-oblique, or Big Shoulders Display italic). Used huge (90–400px) and for UI labels (MENU +, nav links).
- Body: an extended grotesk (F37 Judge Extended; substitute Archivo Expanded) at 10–12px, sentence case.
- Hand-lettered / 3D sticker type for logos. It is imagery, not a font.

## Color usage
- About 45% cream/white, 25% cyan `#00d3ff` (hero type, footer field), 15% black ink, 10% acid lime `#dfff6b` (pills, CTA, gradient), 5% pale cyan `#bcf3ff`.
- Gradients appear only inside the animated lettering (lime → cyan → blue).

## Imagery & iconography
- Kinetic typography, 3D sticker letters, smiley faces, sparkles, lightning squiggles, ribbon tags and brand logos. No photography.
- The cursor is a 3D arrow. The "+" is next to MENU.

## Signature details
1. **Giant italic condensed caps** in cyan bleeding off the viewport.
2. **Morphing logo** cycling lettering styles (gradient bubble / outline / serif / chrome) with stickers.
3. **Chevron-striped lime pill CTA** with a thick outline and offset shadow.
4. **Ghost-grey scrolling service words.**
5. **Cyan footer** with italic caps nav columns flanking the animated logo.

## Interaction & motion
- `cubic-bezier(0.165, 0.84, 0.44, 1)` at ~0.8s. The logo swaps style every ~0.5s. Stripes slide in the CTA. Stickers pop in with overshoot. Hero letters shift on scroll.
- Hover: the CTA squashes and its stripes speed up. Nav words skew.

## Anti-patterns
- No serif body text or thin elegant type in the UI.
- No photography or muted palettes.
- No rounded soft cards. Shapes are pills with hard outlines, or none.
- No static logo. Typography must feel alive.
