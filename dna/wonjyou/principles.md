Source: https://wonjyou.studio/ (Won J. You Studios, personal brand of a veteran designer, professor of UX and design mentor; designed by Huy Phan; Awwwards Site of the Day, Oct 23, 2025 + Developer Award). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A keynote on a dark stage. Black-and-white portraits of Won, huge heavy caps in coral red stacking his roles ("EDUCATOR / COACH / MENTOR / CONSULTANT"), and a warm peach "PROFESSOR" chapter.
- The audience is designers seeking mentorship and companies seeking consulting. The one idea is **confident authority with warmth**: loud typography, intimate monochrome photography, and a handwritten signature.

## Composition & layout
- **Chrome:** a thin **vertical left rail** (~26px) with a chapter index (vertical labels INTRO / MENTORSHIP / ABOUT / EDUCATION…), plus a **vertical orange tab** at the bottom-left "LET'S CONNECT ↗". A small "W" monogram top-left, and social icons top-right later.
- **Hero:** black `#0a0a0a` with a greyscale profile portrait at right fading into black. Stacked coral `#ef5143` caps (~110px, weight 800, tight), staggered left/indented: "EDUCATOR / COACH / MENTOR / CONSULTANT". A tiny caption ("Design mentorship by Won J. You") tucked between lines, and "SCROLL TO DISCOVER ——" at the bottom.
- **Intro statement:** a full-height B&W portrait at left and a cream `#fee9ce` statement at right (~40px bold sentence case, "After years of designing solutions for industry giants and game-changing startups…") with a tiny orange kicker "SAME PASSION. NEW MISSION." and a small coral sticker thumbnail "SELECTED WORKS".
- **Big claim:** a centred coral caps stack (~81px) that **fills from grey to coral line by line on scroll** ("OVER 20 YEARS HELPING STARTUPS DISRUPT MARKETS & FORTUNE 500S INNOVATE AT SCALE"), with a "▶ WATCH INTRO" button and floating project thumbnails.
- **Clients:** "Brand experience" in orange at left, and a big list of brand names in grey (~36px) with the active one in cream. Its **logo appears in a coral square** overlapping the name.
- **Professor chapter:** a peach `#fee9ce` field with the word "PROFESSOR" repeated in **white outline/echo stripes** (sliced repeats above and below) and "You can call me" in coral centred. Then a photo teaching plus a coral 4-line statement "Educating the Next Generation of Design Rebels & Changemakers."
- **Footer:** a B&W moody photo band, contact row ("• SCHEDULE A CALL calendly.com · • LEAVE A MESSAGE 312-… · • EMAIL ME won@…") in cream with orange kickers. The closing quote is centred cream bold ("Research without actionable insight is meaningless.") with an **orange handwritten signature** below, and orange underlined footer links.

## Typography voice
- One geometric-humanist sans (Avantt; substitute Plus Jakarta Sans) at 700–800. Display is **UPPERCASE**, very tight (−0.04em), line-height ~0.92. Statements are bold sentence case.
- Micro text is 9–10px caps bold in orange (`#ffb261`), with bullets "•".
- A script signature (handwritten, orange) as the personal mark.

## Color usage
- About 55% black `#0a0a0a`, 20% coral red `#ef5143` (display type, logo squares), 12% cream/peach `#fee9ce` (statements, Professor field), 6% orange `#ffb261` (tabs, kickers, links, signature), 7% greys `#4e4e4e` (unfilled text).
- Photos are strictly **black and white**.

## Imagery & iconography
- Greyscale portraits (profile, frontal, candid working) with dramatic lighting, fading into black.
- Client logos as white marks inside coral squares. Small project thumbnails as floating stickers.
- Icons: ↗, ▶ in a white circle, and social squares.

## Signature details
1. **Stacked coral heavy caps** listing roles, with staggered indents.
2. **Scroll-fill text**: lines turn from grey to coral as you scroll.
3. **Vertical orange "LET'S CONNECT ↗" tab** and a vertical chapter rail at the left.
4. **Echo/outline repeated word** ("PROFESSOR") on a peach field.
5. **B&W portraits** plus an **orange handwritten signature** under a closing quote.

## Interaction & motion
- `cubic-bezier(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)` at ~0.6s. Scroll-linked colour fill on headlines. Client list highlighting with a logo square that follows. Portraits fade and parallax.
- Echo stripes slide apart vertically as the word settles.

## Anti-patterns
- No colour photography. Photos stay B&W.
- No light-weight or lowercase display type.
- No additional brand colours beyond coral, orange, cream and black.
- No rounded cards or soft shadows.
