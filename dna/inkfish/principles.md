Source: https://inkfishnyc.com/ (Inkfish, a New York creative agency making campaigns and films for brands such as Toyota, ASICS, Nissan and Pfizer; Awwwards Site of the Month, May 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A film editor's index card, blown up. There are tiny monospace lists of clients and numbered films, a strip of bracketed progress bars, video thumbnails with runtimes, and every so often one giant caps statement ("THINKING, SMALL AGENCY ATTITUDE").
- The audience is brand marketers. The one idea is **"big thinking, small agency attitude"**: data-sheet precision in mono, punctuated by bold typographic statements and cinematic footage.

## Composition & layout
- A 4-column grid with a fixed header row: the "inkfish." logo (bold, tight), then tiny mono nav columns ("■ HOME / WORK[17]", "ABOUT / CONTACT", "ONLY THE STRONG / EVOLVE").
- **Hero (black):** two tiny mono lists side by side, clients (TOYOTA, ASICS, NISSAN…) and numbered films ("[01] RAV4 THE WALL", "[02] MIND GAMES"…), with the active row inverted (white bar) and a **segmented progress bar** (█ █ ▌▌▌▌) between. A full-screen video plays behind. "▸ VIEW TOYOTA" sits bottom-left and "SCROLL ↓" (~22px) bottom-right.
- **Work grid (white):** 4-column video thumbnails with no gaps, each with a meta row above (title + client in bold mono, type + runtime "FILM 00:30" right-aligned).
- **About:** centred giant caps "THINKING, / SMALL / AGENCY / ATTITUDE" (~160px, 4 lines, tight), a tiny black pill "MORE ABOUT US", and team bios as bracketed mono at left/right ("[DARREN BORRINO] / CREATIVE LEADER | CONCEPTUAL MARKETER"). Below it, a 4-column mono data table: INFORMATION / SERVICES / CLIENTS / AWARDS ("[16] CANNES LIONS").
- **Contact:** a full-width video band with "CONTACT US" in giant light caps over it, then "↳ BOOK A CALL" (~22px) and the pixel-bar logo mark (black dashes forming "inkfish") next to "ONLY THE / STRONG EVOLVE".
- **Footer:** 4 mono columns with a live clock (01:41:04), address, email and legal.

## Typography voice
- **Display grotesk** (NT Dapper; substitute Archivo) at 400, **UPPERCASE**, 36–160px, tight line-height (~0.85).
- **Martian Mono** at **8–10px UPPERCASE** for everything else: nav, lists, meta, footer. Numbers in square brackets `[01]`, pipes `|` as separators, `↳` and `▸` as bullets.
- The "inkfish." logo is bold lowercase with a registered-mark dot.

## Color usage
- Strictly black and white: black hero, white body, black type. Colour only comes from film stills and video (warm, cinematic).
- The active state is inversion (white bar on black, black bar on white).

## Imagery & iconography
- Campaign film stills and video (people, cars, sports, a cat), arranged in a gapless grid or full-bleed bands. No radius.
- The pixel-bar logo mark is made of black horizontal dashes at different heights.
- Glyph icons only: `■ ▸ ↳ ↓ →`.

## Signature details
1. **Tiny mono index lists** with bracket numbers and an inverted active row.
2. **Segmented progress bar** of black and white blocks showing the reel position.
3. **Giant uppercase statement** centred amid mono data.
4. **Gapless video thumbnail grid** with mono meta rows (title, client, type, runtime).
5. **Pixel-dash logo mark** and a **live clock** in the footer.

## Interaction & motion
- Quick, precise easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`, ~0.3s). Hovering a list row swaps the background film and advances the progress bar. Thumbnails play on hover.
- Headline letters slide up from a mask. The clock ticks.

## Anti-patterns
- No colour in UI, no gradients, no rounded cards (only the tiny pill button).
- No mid-sized body copy. Text is either 8–10px mono or huge display caps.
- No lowercase display headlines, and no serif fonts.
