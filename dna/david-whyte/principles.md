Source: https://davidwhyte.com/experience/ (David Whyte Experience, an immersive companion site for the poet David Whyte's archive and membership; Awwwards Site of the Month, Dec 2024). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- Walking through a misty Yorkshire-style landscape while someone reads you poems. **Black ink-wash clouds** bloom and dissolve to reveal lines of poetry, a lone tree, a stone viaduct and watercolour hills, all on soft paper-grey mist.
- The audience is readers and seekers who want to subscribe to the poet's archive. The one idea is **"a painter's hand working up colour from a dark palette"**: contemplative, slow and literary, where the interface is almost invisible.

## Composition & layout
- A full-viewport scroll experience on warm mist grey `#e8e7e2`, with a soft white fog glow at top.
- A **black ink-wash mask** (a rectangle with torn, smoky edges, ~300×150px at desktop, left-third) holds the poem lines in white serif (~16px, 6–7 lines). It swells and dissolves between stanzas.
- Landscapes (a lone tree silhouette in grey ink, rolling watercolour hills, a viaduct with arches) fade in and out of the mist in the centre and right.
- A small **circle cursor ring** sits mid-screen as a focus point. "Scroll to explore" is bottom-left, "Open the landscape →" is hinted beside the art, and a small up-caret sits bottom-right.
- Content sections: "Subscriber Benefits" (~32px light serif) with sub-items ("Poetry and essays", "Video and talks") as ~20px serif titles plus 10px sans descriptions. Offer rows are light outlined boxes ("Annual Offer with One Week Free Trial" + a white button "Subscribe – $75 / year"). FAQ rows are separated by hairlines, with the question in ~20px light serif and "View" underlined at right.
- The ending is a big "David Whyte" (~52px light serif) beside "Portal", with a "Restart the experience" link and award credits in tiny text.

## Typography voice
- **Canela Text** (substitute Newsreader, light 300) for all headings and poetry: light weight, sentence case, graceful, 20–86px. Italic for quotations.
- **Roobert** (substitute Inter) at 10–12px for descriptions, links and credits, understated and underlined for links.
- Poems in the ink mask are white serif with a faint outline glow so they read over the textured black.

## Color usage
- About 60% mist grey/paper (`#e8e7e2`, `#efeae2`), 25% black ink, 10% muted watercolour landscape tones (moss `#958e09`, heather, stone, a touch of amber `#d8914c`), 5% white (buttons, fog).
- An occasional violet `#720eec` appears only in media, not UI. There are no saturated UI colours.

## Imagery & iconography
- Ink-wash / smoke textures as masks and transitions, and watercolour or photo-painted landscapes (tree, hills, viaduct) that emerge from mist with soft edges.
- Minimal icons: a thin circle ring, a small caret, and underlined text links.

## Signature details
1. **Black ink-wash window** with torn smoky edges, revealing white serif poetry.
2. **Landscape vignettes emerging from mist** (lone tree, viaduct, hills) as the page scrolls.
3. **Light serif (Canela) for everything spoken**, with a tiny sans for everything functional.
4. **Hairline FAQ rows and outlined offer boxes** with small white buttons, quiet commerce.
5. **"Restart the experience"** ending with a big light serif name.

## Interaction & motion
- Very slow and fluid (0.8–1.5s ease). The ink mask grows and dissolves between stanzas, and landscapes cross-fade through fog. Text fades line by line.
- The cursor ring follows the mouse gently. Links underline on hover. No bounce and no snappy transitions.

## Anti-patterns
- No bold headlines, bright colours or gradient buttons.
- No hard-edged image frames. Everything emerges from mist or ink.
- No dense layouts or card grids. Keep one poem or one idea per screen.
- No playful or techy UI. Keep it literary and quiet.
