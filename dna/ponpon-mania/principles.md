Source: https://ponpon-mania.com/ (Ponpon Mania, an interactive web comic and music EP about a sheep called Marny; Awwwards Site of the Month, Oct 2025). A single website reference, so there is no blend.

## Essence
- A Saturday-morning cartoon has turned into a record shop. Chunky outlined characters (a sheep, a wolf, a dinosaur) party inside a blob-shaped window, and the story continues as a crate of album covers you flip through while tracks play.
- The audience is comic and music fans and the design community. The one idea is **"your interactive comic"**: playful, loud and tactile, with every UI element feeling like a sticker, a record sleeve or a speech bubble.

## Composition & layout
- **Home/cover:** a periwinkle `#7d7dfd` field holding one huge **blob-shaped frame** (a cloud or flower silhouette with a thick black outline) that contains a detailed sunset illustration. Characters overflow its edge, and pink clouds bleed off the bottom.
- The top bar is centred: "chapters" (pill with outline) · the stacked two-line wordmark "ponpon / mania" · "about". Language "EN · FR" sits top-right.
- The primary CTA sits bottom-centre over the art: a **black pill "read now"** (~18px bold lowercase, white).
- A rotating circular sticker badge ("YOUR INTERACTIVE COMIC!" around a flower icon) is pinned at the frame's top-right.
- **Chapters/music view:** a full-screen **radial gradient** background (periwinkle ↔ red ↔ orange, grainy) with a central album cover (square, slightly tilted neighbours in 3D perspective, plastic-wrap sheen). A black rounded **player pill** under it shows the track number and title ("01.welcome to Marny"), EP name, time, and ▶ ⏮ ⏭. Locked tracks show a padlock.
- A small "support us" outline pill sits bottom-right and a 2×2 dot grid icon bottom-left.

## Typography voice
- One family (Libre Franklin), used heavy (800–900) for the wordmark and titles and bold for UI. **Everything is lowercase**, including buttons, nav and track titles.
- The wordmark is a stacked, tightly leaded (line-height ~0.8) chunky lowercase lockup.
- UI text is small (10–13px) and friendly. Cover art uses hand-lettered bubble and graffiti titles ("WOW", "SCRATCH THAT!", "Marny") as imagery.

## Color usage
- A saturated cartoon palette on dark UI chrome: periwinkle `#7d7dfd`, tangerine `#fdaf22`, bubblegum pink `#f191ba`, vermilion `#e74f1f`, lime `#4cb247`, cream `#feece3`, and near-black `#171717` for outlines, pills and the cookie bar.
- Backgrounds are either flat periwinkle or **grainy radial gradients** mixing two to three of those hues. Each chapter or track shifts the gradient.
- Black is used for line work and UI pills, never as a page background.

## Imagery & iconography
- Hand-drawn cartoon illustration: thick uniform black outlines, flat fills with simple cel shading, expressive faces, and city/sunset backdrops.
- Album covers are square with a sticker circle in a corner, cellophane reflection, and slight rotation in 3D space.
- Icons are simple and filled (play, skip, padlock, dot grid). The circular text badge rotates slowly.

## Signature details
1. **Blob-shaped illustration window** with a thick black outline on a flat periwinkle field.
2. **Black lowercase pills** for all actions ("read now", "chapters", player bar).
3. **Album-cover carousel in 3D** with a player pill: story chapters as tracks.
4. **Grainy radial gradients** in cartoon hues behind content.
5. **Rotating circular sticker badge** and the stacked chunky lowercase wordmark.

## Interaction & motion
- Bouncy overshoot easing (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`), 0.3–0.6s. Characters idle-animate (blink, bob), the badge spins, and clouds drift.
- Covers swing and tilt in 3D as you scroll or drag between tracks, and the background gradient morphs per track.
- Pills squash slightly on press. Sound is part of the experience (music playback).

## Anti-patterns
- No sharp rectangles for UI. Use pills and blobs. Covers are the only squares.
- No thin or elegant typography, no uppercase titles, and no serif fonts.
- No photography and no realistic 3D. Keep everything illustrated.
- No muted or neutral palettes, and no pure white page backgrounds.
